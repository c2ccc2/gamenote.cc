How a Metroidvania Map Is Organized

Start with recognizable landmarks, ability requirements and return routes, rather than a map that merely looks good.

2026-09-26

A map is not finished when every room has been drawn. It needs to explain where the player is, what is missing and what routes are possible next.

Start with things players recognize.

Area names are not always the most effective entry point. A landmark in a screenshot, a newly obtained ability, a door or a mechanism often better matches the question a player brings.

Ability requirements matter more than completion percentages.

A useful map clearly distinguishes places that can be reached now from places to revisit later, reducing aimless backtracking.

Connect routes to the guide.

A map should lead to the relevant walkthrough, boss and collectible pages, while those pages should lead back to the map. This two-way relationship is more useful than an isolated large image.
