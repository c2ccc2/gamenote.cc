After Islets: The Hardest Part of a Guide Is Not the Bosses

The challenge is turning “Where should I go now?” into a clear route at every stage of play.

2026-09-24

Boss tactics can often be explained on their own. The more complicated problem is moving from one area to the next with incomplete clues.

Different questions on the same map.

A first visit, a return after obtaining a new ability and a collectible cleanup run do not need the same answers. The walkthrough must distinguish them.

Make the next step recognizable.

Instead of naming only a place, provide visible landmarks, the immediate objective and ability requirements. Players should not need to identify their chapter before they can continue.
