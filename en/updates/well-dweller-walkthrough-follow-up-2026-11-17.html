Well Dweller guide update — Later routes and completion checks

2026-11-17

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-09-27

Seven later areas now have route diagrams, all six quests link to their key steps, and boss follow-ups, collectible links and patch notes have been expanded.

Later routes and gameplay recordings

Seven later areas now have route diagrams in both languages. Those seven areas, all 13 boss fights, six quests and ten time trials link to the original gameplay index and timestamps. In-guide gameplay screenshots for those areas and step-by-step verification are still pending.

Quests and the true ending

The quest index links to key steps for the six quests, including the lift parts, missing sailors, Graven Valley rescue and castle cages. The true-ending guide connects the Queen’s chambers, Soar, Mysterious Markings and all five Spirit Fragment entrances, with tips for the Docks challenge to collect 100 lights.
