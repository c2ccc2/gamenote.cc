Phantom Blade Zero guide complete

2026-11-15

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-09-27

The guide covers the main walkthrough, side quests, boss strategies, weapons, key collectibles and the true ending route.

From Weeping Creek Valley to Soulbreak Mountain

In Against the Flow, cross the log by the bell, climb the ladder and follow the chains and wall-run route to the rope. Speak to Yu’er below to start the Flesh Lily quest. Take the branch above the waterfall if you want Sanguine Twin first.

Check side quests before major fights

After Peng Meng, return to camp for Yu’er’s Apprentice Healer quest and turn in all three herbs before fighting Jing Ye. After Bride Ahua, collect the Flesh Lily at the altar and give it to Yu’er, preferably before fighting Bai Chilian.

Boss strategies and weapon locations

Use the boss archive to check arena approaches and attack responses. In the valley, look to the right of the camp blacksmith for Sanguine Reach. Jagged Steel and Dread Axe come from Jagged Steel and Peng Meng respectively.

Open related section →

Key collectibles and true ending checkpoints

Before entering Dream Manor, keep a separate save at the Green Snake House gate in Pang Town and check earlier purple-marker quests. Complete the fifth dragon pillar challenge in the manor and check Bloodline Awakening at The Last Chance bell in Soulbreak Mountain. Revisit unfinished pillars before entering the ice cave. Exact cutoffs for some quests still need confirmation.
