EDITORIAL VIEW

Our take

After completing the PC and mobile versions, the map structure, movement and ability designs stand out, as does the care taken with the mobile port. Yet hyperarmor and red attacks restrict combos, excellent abilities such as the grapple and Chakram shadow arrive too late, and Athra resources and boss mechanics further constrain the payoff. The quality and care are visible, but the experience still feels like a missed opportunity.

BEFORE YOU START

Who it suits

You may enjoy it if

Players who enjoy connected maps, ability-based revisits, demanding platforming and skill combinations, including those who value thoughtful touchscreen controls.

Keep in mind

Frequent platforming, boss patterns that reward memorization and limited attack windows. The full review discusses late-game abilities and the ending.
