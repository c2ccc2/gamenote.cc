Castlevania: Belmont’s Curse demo is available

Konami has released a playable demo ahead of the planned October 15, 2026 launch.

Konami announced on October 1, 2026 that a demo of Castlevania: Belmont’s Curse is available on PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, Xbox Series X|S and Steam. It covers the early part of the game.

According to Konami, demo save data can carry over to the full game when played on the same platform and account, provided the demo save is retained. The full release is scheduled for October 15, 2026. See the official announcement below for the transfer conditions.
