Secrets at the Heart of the Lake

Petal I · Leave Zhuo Bi’s arena through the left exit and inspect the blue flower near the children in the lantern courtyard. Any petal starts the quest. Track the purple objective in the journal.

Petal II · From the Well of the Three Puppets bell, find the ledge above the stream. Drop below the bridge, follow the water to the end and clear the enemies by the flower.

Petal III · At the Covered Bridge bell, enter the house on the right and descend to the open ground. Look past the villagers for the flower beside the shrine in front of the house.

Petal IV · At the Engine Giant Remains bell, enter the house ahead and drop through the floor opening to the bottom. Go outside, head left over the log and look for the flower at the end.

Petal V · Once the red threads clear, cross the Covered Bridge and pass the fallen log. Turn right before the next stairs, climb down the ladder inside the house and follow the lower path to the cliff edge.

Petal VI · From the Temple of Blue Fireflies bell, enter the house with blue lanterns. The flower sits on the furniture to your right. Collecting all six increases maximum HP by 10%.

After collecting all six, return to the house for petal VI and exit through the rear door into the canyon. Follow it to the Broken Puppet Pit Entrance bell and drop from the wooden platform beside it to fight Dusk. He drops the Puppeteer’s Bell and the Illusory Pipa Head.

Yanru’s mask · From the Terraces bell in the Village of Lu, look in the shallow pond for the red scarecrow under the lantern tree. Interact to start the fight.

Guhe’s mask · Land at Meigu Village East Dock and follow the village’s main road to the green scarecrow by a yellow-leafed tree.

Lei Ting’s mask · Burn the brambles by Pangzhen’s Pond of Fading Song bell with Flaming Mane. Cross the slums, climb the ladder to Old Man Gen’s House bell, then follow the water downhill to the talisman scarecrow.

Three illusion gates · Look northeast of the Puppet Testing Grounds bell in the Village of Lu, northwest of Training Grounds Depths in the Mandate Encampment and southeast of Puppet Cell on the Mountain of Imprisonment. Each entrance opens a separate arch inside the illusion.

The mountain entrance requires the repaired Illusory Pipa. Get the head from Dusk, the backplate from a table beyond the Mandate Encampment’s final boss and the strings from Yan Qiansui at the sunken opera house in southern Meigu Lake. Take all three to Myst in Pangzhen’s underground tavern.

Yin eye and Puppet’s Tongue · Once all three arches are open, interact with Three-Eyed Puppet No. 66 in the center. Take the tongue and Crystal Puppet Eye [Yin] before returning to Weeping Creek Valley.

Yang eye and Hansui’s mask · Ride the hidden lift under the Weeping Creek Valley Sha-Chi Tower down to Crimson Manor and fit the tongue into the puppet lock. Circle the ground floor to the rear and drop into the arena. Hansui joins the Puppet Scavenger fight. Check for the Yang eye and Hansui’s mask afterward.

With all four masks, head north from the Frozen Lake Dock bell in northwestern Meigu Lake to the Four Guardians’ Mirror. Use it to enter the ruins. On return trips the mirror can send you to the first bell or the last one you rested at.

Ruins entrance · Climb the snowy log ramp from the Phantom Illusionist Ruins bell, drop to the plank deck and slide down the chain at the far end. Check the purple glow beside the red skeletons for the Master Healer’s Memory, used for the final Medicine upgrade.

Approaching the ice · Continue through the ruins to the Forbidden Path bell and head for the frozen lake wreck. The Whiteshadow’s Fall bell is on the wreck’s deck. The fight can trigger on approach, so ring the bell and rest first if you reach the deck in time.

Blizzard Blademaster · Back away from close-range ice spikes. Dodge on the red flash and wait for the airborne slam to land before moving back in. When red sparks mark the spreading ice ring, move away from the boss. Victory awards Whiteshadow and spawns Puppet No. 99 on the ice.

Entering the ritual · Rest at Whiteshadow’s Fall and confirm both Crystal Puppet Eyes. Interact with Puppet No. 99 on the ice to insert both at once and go straight into the ritual encounter.

Ritual encounter · Approach the kneeling ritual participants one at a time. Empty each target’s health bar before moving to the next. When the last one falls, the quest completion banner appears and Forbidden Arts Beneath the Lake unlocks.

Ending check · The cutscene places you next to Inanis. Speak to her until you receive Violet Blossom. This conversation counts toward the ending requirements. Finish before entering the ice cave past The Last Chance bell on Mount Soulrend.

Reward use · Violet Blossom unlocks the Nirvana Soul Temper for Inanis’ Radiant Fan. Taking fatal damage inside the fan’s field revives you once with 30% HP. Touch a bell or respawn to reset the effect.

Leaving the Realm of Mirage · Cross to the blue-flower islet opposite Inanis. Circle to the side facing away from her and approach the portal until the interaction prompt appears.
