Dreamplay Manor

Area at a glance

Progression goal
Enter by the red-lit stairs, pass the lantern shore, defeat Qiufeng in the candlelit hall, then take the double doors to the courtyard.
Bosses
Yuding and Qiufeng.
Drops
Yuding rewards the two-handed crescent blade.
Side quests and clues
Collect the three memories at the arena gallery, the candlelit hall and the red-lit shrine.
Items and services
Candlelit hall: second Three-Faced Puppet memory. All three grant +7% health.

Dreamplay Manor: entrance stairs

Follow the stone stairs uphill between the red lanterns.

Dreamplay Manor: lantern shore

At the floating lanterns, use the lit building across the water to orient yourself.

Yuding: lantern arena and crescent blade

From the lantern shore, look for the wide timber arena with strings of red lanterns and a large drum at the edge. Yuding carries a green-bladed polearm.

Yuding’s polearm can sweep into reach from mid-range. When the blade moves to the side, stop advancing and keep room to move laterally. Wait for the sweep and recovery before closing for a short combo.

After the finisher, the two-handed crescent blade appears on the equipment prompt. Choose whether to equip it there.

After closing the reward screen, follow the red cords at the arena edge to the wooden stairs. On the gallery, check the chest beside the screen, then find the blue flower along the patterned railing overlooking the drum. This is the first memory.

Candlelit hall: Qiufeng and the memory quest

Find the hall hung with white drapes off the outer walkway. Use the altar at the far end to orient yourself. Qiufeng fights inside this petal-strewn hall.

Qiufeng follows the first white blade arc with another quick cut. Defend through the sequence and counter briefly once he stops. If pushed against the latticed doors, move back into the middle of the hall for room to evade sideways.

After the fight, stay inside and follow the petals toward the altar. If you finished the fight by the latticed doors, turn back toward the centre aisle and look for the hanging drapes.

At the altar, look between the scattered paper offerings and round cushions for the glowing blue flower. Inspect it and let the dialogue finish to reach the memory collection screen.

Confirm the second memory of the Three-Faced Puppet. The recording shows 2/3 and lists a +7% health bonus for completing the set. Once collected, check which other memories you still need.

Close the memory screen and check the quest update in the upper right. It directs you to look for the remaining flowers in Dreamplay Manor. The footage shows 2/3; use your own quest counter to track what remains.

After collecting the memory, follow the edge of the hall to the dark double doors with two upright wooden handles. Open them to reach the paved courtyard with white drapes and continue exploring.

Dreamplay Manor: carved plinth

Inspect the blue flower at the base of the carved stone plinth.

Three-Faced Puppet: all three memories

The first flower is on the gallery overlooking Yuding's wooden arena. Find the large drum beyond the patterned railing, inspect the flower on the low stand, then confirm 1/3 after the dialogue.

After defeating Qiufeng inside the white-draped hall, follow the petals to the altar and inspect the flower among the cushions and paper offerings. Check for 2/3, then leave by the double doors.

The third flower is in the red-lit shrine. Look for latticed windows, rows of offering dishes and round cushions, then inspect the flower at the tiered altar. Close the memory screen and check that the objective is crossed out. The completed set grants +7% health.
