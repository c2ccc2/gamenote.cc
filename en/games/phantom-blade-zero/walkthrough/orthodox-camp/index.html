Orthodox Camp: blue flower by the window

Find the open window overlooking the large roots. Inspect the blue glow beside the candle to hear Mo Jizi’s dialogue.

Beyond Orthodox Camp

For the Baishan tale, defeat Wu Lengshuang and inspect the body among the flowers before Yubi. Defeating Red on your first encounter fails her tale.

View quest requirements → Baishan investigation order → Red’s quest route →

Mass Graves. Follow the narrow trail between the high rock walls toward the sunlit gap.

Battlefield Ruins. Enter the timber-walled platform and inspect the red-lit stand.

Three Towers. Follow the planks to the rock wall and inspect the blue flower in the shade for dialogue.

Place of Confinement. Use the brazier to identify the junction and follow the trail on its right around the rock wall.

Training Ground. Inspect the blue flower along the wooden platform, then check the red-and-gold chest to the right.

At the Training Ground, inspect the flower left of the red-and-gold chest. After the dialogue, confirm the Experiment Victim memory, then open the chest on the terrace facing the bare tree.

The Training Ground chest contains a medicine with three uses. Each use restores 20% health and grants 10% increased damage for 30 seconds.
