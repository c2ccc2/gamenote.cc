Pang Town

Area at a glance

Progression goal
From the lantern balcony, follow the market and backstreets past the ancestral hall toward the docks. The tower and hall have return routes.
Bosses
The recordings index Fire Meteor, Wu Xuan, Xie Qiudong and Changhong. Fight details are pending.

Pang Town: red-lantern balcony

On the upper balcony overlooking the tiled roofs, follow the red lanterns and pass through the opening with lanterns on both sides.

Through Pang Town

Market. After passing the market buildings, climb the broad stone stairs toward the daylight in the gateway.

Phantom Tavern. Speak to the person at the counter in front of the jar shelves. A pale flask marks the counter.

Eight Trigrams Pavilion. Speak to the blue-robed figure by the stone steps, beside the circular carving and pillar.

Backstreet Archway. On the tiled roof, follow the wooden wall on the right. Rows of blue cloth below identify the backstreet rooftops.

Narrow Lane Houses. Inside the wooden house with hanging red strips, check the purple pickup glow beyond the railing.

Three Ships Well. Follow the dark tunnel to the brazier and take the lit opening to its left.

Green Snake House. Look for the long counter and wall of small drawers to identify this interior.

Ancestral Hall Square. From the brazier and stacked barrels under the eaves, take the broad steps on the right.

Sealed Grounds. Cross the courtyard and climb the long stairs toward the red-lit hall.

Dock Warehouse. Follow the stone-railed crossing toward the far buildings, with lily pads on either side.

Rear Mountain Pavilion. Follow the mountain path to the overlook and inspect the red-lit wooden stand.

From the upper balcony, step onto the tiled roof. Follow the eaves lined with jars and cross the slanted plank between the roofs. Enter the room with red strips and collect the amber fragment at the altar. Leave through the sunlit side doorway to return to the lane.

Return to the Green Snake House with the three components. Interact with the double doors with painted guardians and a protruding snake head. Watch for the component-use notices, then follow the passage toward Dreamplay Manor after the mechanism lifts.

Pang Town Sha-Chi Tower: red-lit stairs

Inside the tower, locate the short staircase facing the red relief. Draw enemies down to the wider lower level before climbing to the landing.

Pang Town revisit: red-lit hall

On revisiting Pang Town, climb the broad red-lit steps toward the hall for dialogue.
