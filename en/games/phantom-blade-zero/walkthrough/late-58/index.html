Snow Mountain

Area at a glance

Progression goal
Climb the cliffside ramp and follow the exposed planks beyond the brazier.
Bosses
Snowstorm Swordsman, on the snowfield beneath the ropes.
Drops
The Snowstorm Swordsman drops Baiying, which builds up freeze on hit.
Side quests and clues
The recording indexes the Dragon Vein and Master Ye. Triggers are pending.

Pending verification

Snow Mountain: cliffside ramp

Climb the snowy wooden ramp toward the blue-green glow.

Snow Mountain: whiteout path

Leave the brazier and use the exposed planks to stay on the path in the snow.

Snow Mountain: checkpoint, Snowstorm Swordsman and Baiying

Find the blue flower in the snowy grass below the ramp and inspect it for dialogue.

For the edict, follow the snow beneath the timber platforms back toward the boat and inspect the pickup on the planks beside it. Confirm it in the Holy Mother mystery records before continuing.

At the ice cave mouth with two hanging cages, activate the Forbidden Path checkpoint at the red-lit stand.

Beyond the checkpoint, follow the broken planks over the water. Use the red drapes beneath the ice wall and the cave opening on the right to identify the junction.

On the side route, find the short jetty with a lantern post and stacked crates. Inspect the purple pickup before the crates, then explore the blue-lit water edge. The pickup notification shows four pharmacist memories.

After the pickup, follow the boards to the flower by the water. Finish the dialogue for the sixth Snowstorm Swordsman memory. The recording reaches 6/6; the full set grants +10% health.

The Snowstorm Swordsman fights on the snowfield beneath the ropes. Give yourself lateral space when he approaches glowing red, and return to open snow after short combos.

The finisher yields the two-handed weapon Baiying. Its equipment screen says hits build up freeze and freeze enemies when the meter fills.
