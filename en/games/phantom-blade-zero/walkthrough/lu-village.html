Lu Village

Area highlights

Goal
From the village, follow the cave, lakeshore, well and bridge route to the Puppet Testing Ground and lakeside.
Bosses
Zhuo Bi guards the village entrance. Collect all six Firefly memories to reach Danghui through the canyon behind the Temple of Blue Fireflies.
Materials
Blackstone Shards 4 · Amber Shards 4
Items
Puppeteer’s Bell, pipa head, Lan’s quest accessory and cellar documents
Rewards
Danghui drops the Puppeteer’s Bell and the Illusory Pipa Head.
Side content
Accept Dahuang’s tale inside the house near the Survivors’ Courtyard bell. Deliver the Songstress Puppet’s letter from the Temple of Blue Fireflies to the man in purple.

One amber shard is on an adjoining route.

Lu Village: accessory polishing

Look for the red workbench and orange lantern. Speak to the person wearing round glasses and select accessory polishing.

Lu Village walkthrough: from the entrance to the lakeshore

Head from the village entrance to the lakeshore. Finish the timed quest steps before fighting Mu Xiaokui, collect the dock and tree-side loot afterward, then return to report to Lan and the old man.

View quest requirements → The Journey Home → Young Master Lan → Five headband locations → A Puppet’s Wooden Heart route →

From the village entrance to the lakeside

Mother Statue Cave approach. Climb the mossy steps through the bamboo toward the wooden gate flanked by lanterns.

If you just defeated Zhuo Bi, open the arena chest at the village entrance for one Blackstone Shard. Location reference ↗

Lakeside Arch Bridge overlook. Follow the cliffside trail to the spreading tree and chain railing. Use the buildings across the ravine to orient yourself.

Search the well and covered bridge, then take the bandit passage

Three-Puppet Well. Listen to the white puppet beside the red cords and candles. For the amber, take the lower branch down to the river before continuing to the covered bridge.

Go down to the river and turn right. Clear the enemies in front of the chest, then return to the crying person and talk to them for one Amber Shard. Location reference ↗

Covered Bridge. Follow the candles inside the covered bridge toward the red-lit shrine, checking the inner passage at the railing corner.

Clear the enemies past the shrine on the lower level and inspect the coffin beneath the tree for one Blackstone Shard. After crossing the bridge, take the right fork, enter the house and go down the ladder to the outer cliff for one Amber Shard.

Bandit Passage. Check the blue flowers left of the red-lit doorway, beside the seated figure and white candles.

Defeat the remnant at the passage entrance and open the purple chest on the right for one Blackstone Shard.

Climb the terraces and check the courtyard and temple quests

Terraces. At the end of the planks, look for the ladder on the left side of the scaffold against the rock wall.

Survivors’ Courtyard. Enter the house by the bell to accept Dahuang’s request. If you have the puppet performer’s letter, follow the wall to the person in purple and hand it over. Otherwise, return after checking the temple.

Temple of Blue Fireflies. Check the glow beneath the roots by the blue-lit wall. For the memory quest, enter the house with blue lanterns by the bell and inspect the flower on the furniture just to the right. With all six memories, take the rear exit into the canyon to fight Danghui. For the main route, continue to the testing ground.

Cross the testing ground and fight Mu Xiaokui

Puppet Testing Ground. Climb the red-lit stairs beside the row of grates. Puppets hang from red cords on the nearby rock wall.

After opening the passage to Pang Town, enter the room at the end and inspect the purple glow on the table for one Blackstone Shard.

Before fighting Mu Xiaokui

  1. Hand all three treasures to Quick-Hand Zhang and claim the thread ball. Report the wounded-thief quest and check for the arrow-protection charm.
  2. Accept Lan’s quest and progress it to the passage-opening step. After the fight, open the passage, return to report to Lan and the old man, then enter Pang Town.
  3. Speak to Xiao Cui for her red headband. Continue her quest later in Cage Mountain.
  4. Check all five red ribbons. The supplied route notes link them to the later phase and the saw weapon reward.
  5. Refill medicine and consumables. The supplied loadout uses the timing accessory, Boulderguard and the ring, or a survival charm. Save charged axe attacks for clear openings.

Lakeshore. The reed field between the leaning tree and the distant roof is Mu Xiaokui’s arena. Finish the checklist before entering, fight in the open, and save charged axe attacks for clear openings. After the fight, search the dock and the area under the tree before taking the lift.

After Mu Xiaokui: before the lift

  1. Collect the sword at the dock and open the chest under the tree for an amber shard and a page.
  2. The supplied route notes state that the scene collapses after leaving by lift, preventing a return for these pickups. Collect them before departing.
  3. After opening the passage, travel back to Lu Village and report to Lan and the old man before progressing farther into Pang Town.
  4. Other ordinary village chests and records can be revisited. Xiao Cui’s Cage Mountain follow-up can wait.

Optional exploration: the bandit treasure cellar

Bandit Treasure Cellar. Follow the firelight to the barred wooden doorway and listen to the person behind it.

  • Bandit Cellar: prisoner record · After the Blackstone stand, enter the timber hall and find the round table with cups and a red candle before the shelves. Inspect the document for the prisoner record in the puppet-base ledger collection.
  • Mengcha document: unfinished scripture · After the prisoner record on the round table, follow the shelves of scrolls and inspect the glowing document on the floor. Its collection screen identifies an unfinished scripture.
  • Mengcha document: arrest order · In the same cellar study, inspect the low desk before the hanging painting and small altar. Confirm the arrest-order document in the collection screen before leaving.

After the room with stacked books, follow the lamp on the stone wall and descend the wooden slope past the red post. Check the chest against the wall, then follow the candles to the second chest by the shrine.

Danghui: Temple of Blue Fireflies

Danghui: Temple of Blue Fireflies →

Follow the planks by the blue-lit rock wall into the Temple of Blue Fireflies. The red candles clustered by the stone ledges mark the approach to the masked puppeteer.

The puppets have health bars of their own alongside Danghui’s. If several surround you, back into the open gravel and keep them in view. Counter after the immediate attacks finish and avoid committing to a long combo by the cave wall.

After the fight, confirm the pipa head story item and stay to hear the kneeling puppeteer explain the token. Once you have the backplate and strings, take all three parts to Myst in Pangzhen’s underground tavern.

  • Pipa head: Danghui story item · Defeat Danghui in the Temple of Blue Fireflies and check the story item prompt. Listen to the kneeling puppeteer explain the token. Its later hand-in location is pending verification.
