Soulbreak Mountain

Area highlights

Goal
Pass the icicles and snowy slope, then follow the pale ground toward the figure in the red realm.
Bosses
The paired fight comes earlier. At the summit, face the Herald and then the Holy Mother. The ending branch determines whether Zuo Shang follows.
Materials
Blackstone Shards 0 · Amber Shards 0
Items
Bell tempering material, paired fans and their tempering material; true-ending sword and story item
Rewards
The Herald drops the Puppeteer’s Bell tempering material. The Holy Mother drops her paired fan and its tempering material.
Side content
Before entering the final ice cave, speak to Inanis after the lake ritual, receive Violet Blossom and confirm all five pillar trials are complete. Both requirements unlock the true ending route.

Soulbreak Mountain: snowy approach

After the icicles, follow the snowy uphill trail between the dark rock walls.

Ice cavern: the paired encounter

Strongman and Sawyer: ice cavern →

Continue from the snowy slope into the blue cavern. After the puppet’s entrance, leave room to move near the centre.

Later the health bar changes to the Strongman’s transformed state. Move laterally when the red chained ball comes out and close in after it passes.

Cui: prison rescue and Mount Soulrend follow-up →

Soulbreak Mountain: red realm

In the red realm, follow the pale ground between the dark ridges and approach the figure for dialogue.

Finale: Mengcha and the Holy Mother

Before entering the final ice cave on Mount Soulrend, confirm all five pillar trials are complete and you received Violet Blossom from Inanis after the lake ritual.

View quest requirements → Five pillar challenges → Lake ritual and the Inanis conversation →

Holy Mother: red realm →Leng Tu, Herald of the Veil →

Follow the pale ground to the figure in the red realm. When Lengcha attacks with white arcs at close range, get through the string before countering.

When the Holy Mother takes over, watch the blue-purple glow as she rises. Move sideways while keeping her in view, and close in after the action ends.

After the Holy Mother fight, follow the pale path between the dark ridges toward the golden pillar.

Before climbing the pale slope to the golden pillar, save at the wooden stand on the right. Walk into the pillar to reach the dark scene with a hall ahead.

Ice cave to summit · After choosing to continue at The Last Chance, follow the stormy path uphill past your allies and rest at the Soulrend Cliff bell. Follow the snowy path beyond it to face the Herald of the Veil, then the Merciful Lady of the Blossom Veil.

After the Lady · If the ending starts immediately, this is the final encounter for that route. If the path remains open, rest at The Fallen Dream bell and interact with the golden pillar farther uphill. After the transition, follow the dark lakeshore toward the pavilion to trigger Zuo Shang.

After Zuo Shang · On the Lóng’s Roar route, the ending plays after defeating Zuo Shang. If he transforms into the True Lóng, finish the second phase and follow the final sword-clash prompt to reach the Final Battle ending card and With Heart and Blood achievement.

Zuo Shang: night courtyard and dragon form

Zuo Shang: night courtyard and dragon form →

Save at the checkpoint below the golden pillar, then walk into the light. In the dark scene beyond it, approach the hall and finish the courtyard dialogue to start the fight.

Before the fight, ring the broken Phantom of The Old House bell by the lake. On defeat, return along the shore from that bell. If the lake quest is complete but the pillar trials are unfinished, the fight ends after phase one.

Phase one · Create space when Zuo Shang rises above the water and gathers gold light. Leave room to dodge sideways when he lengthens the blade, then close in for a short punish after the thrust passes. Keep moving while golden swords fall and wait for the last one before stopping.

In the night courtyard, Zuo Shang can send out a low blue ring. When it forms in front of him, stay outside its path and close in once it dissipates.

After the transition, the health bar identifies Zuo Shang’s dragon form. He can follow the golden weapon effects with a red close-range slash. Leave room to evade sideways and counter briefly after the arc passes.

Phase two · Move sideways out of the beam that travels across the lake. Track his landing point when he dives sword-first. If the dive ends in a blade lock, press the input displayed between the blades promptly.

After emptying the health bar, watch for the onscreen input. The recording displays B on a controller. Follow the prompt for your device to finish the sequence and reach the cutscene.

Final Battle rewards · After the credits, return to the Realm of Mirage and check for the one-handed sword Aureate, its Soul Tempering material Broken Lóng Body and the story item Radiant Heart.
