Major characters from official material. Appearances, interactions and deeper story connections will be added with spoiler labels.

Reference cards · details still being checked

Khazan

A former imperial general, framed and banished to the mountains; the gameundefineds protagonist.

  • Khazan was a general of the Pell Los Empire and a hero of the battle against the Berserk Dragon.
  • After being framed, tortured and banished, his survival begins the revenge story.

Source ↗

Blade Phantom

A phantom from the Netherworld that forms an alliance with Khazan.

  • Blade Phantom comes from the Netherworld and seeks a vessel able to contain its power.
  • Its alliance with Khazan underpins both the story and Phantom Form.

Source ↗

Ozma

A hero who fought the Berserk Dragon alongside Khazan and later fell under treason accusations.

  • Ozma fought the Berserk Dragon alongside Khazan.
  • The official profile says he also fell from grace after treason accusations; later story details will carry spoiler labels.

Source ↗

Quimuk

A Bantu shaman who rescued Khazan. Related appearances and missions will be added.

  • Quimuk is the Bantu shaman who rescued Khazan from the snowy mountains.
  • Her official profile says she proposed a Bantu ritual to drive out the phantom within him.

Source ↗

Daphrona

Met beneath the Forgotten Temple; offers Soulstone unleashing.

  • Speak to her near the Nexus after descending by elevator.
  • After collecting Soulstones, check her service menu for your preferred upgrades.
