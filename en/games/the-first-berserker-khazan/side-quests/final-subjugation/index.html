The Final Subjugation

Guide editor: GAME NOTE

GAME NOTE CLASSIC · GUIDE LIBRARYThe First Berserker: Khazan

Continue Xilence’s mission chain after Human Xilence.

01Abandoned Castle bridge: speak to Xilence and climb the stairs

Begin the newly unlocked Final Subjugation side mission.

Speak to Xilence on arrival.

Cross the bridge ahead.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

Climb the ladder and go right for a Soulstone below.

03Room left of the Soulstone: scroll in the explosive chest

Return after the Soulstone and absorb the memory ahead.

  • Chaos Erosion Scroll · Location list

The room left of the Soulstone contains an exploding chest with a red Chaos Erosion Scroll.

05Slope beyond the carts: search the ledges and enter the right doorway to save

Break the cart and continue forward.

Another cart on the right conceals a blue item.

Collect the blue item on the platform ahead.

Descend and enter the right doorway for a Blade Nexus.

06Clearing outside the Nexus: collect the memory and jar on the left

Absorb the memory in the open area outside.

  • Jarling · Location list

The pot ahead on the left contains a Jarling.

08Nexus ahead: look up for the Soulstone and search the left doorway chest

Open the chest on the right.

Continue to the Blade Nexus.

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

Look up to the left for a Soulstone overhead.

Open the chest through the left doorway.

10Castle boss room: face Xilence under the scale's influence

Enter the boss room to face Xilence, corrupted by the scale. Its moves resemble Viper's later form without new attacks.

Victory grants equipment and Human Xilence's Scale, completing the mission.
