Why Did You Abandon Us

01Tristan's request: return to the laser laboratory

Back at the hub, speak to Yulian to unlock another area.

Speak to Tristan beside him and accept Why Did You Abandon Us.

Beyond the red portal, a new staircase opens on the left.

The side mission remains at the original right-hand portal.

Rest after entering.

02Lasers and cell switches: use the beams against enemies and open the gates

Use the lasers against the enemies ahead by maneuvering around their beams, rather than fighting every enemy directly.

After clearing them, activate the switch to open the nearby door.

Enter this doorway.

Clear the undead and activate the next switch to open the left iron gate.

Collect the purple equipment inside the cell.

A white item lies in the first cell.

03Bookshelf passage below: operate the switch for the Warrior Scroll

Descend and collect the blue item.

A bookshelf in the corner conceals a passage.

Enter and activate the mechanism on the left.

  • Warrior Scroll · Location list

Collect the Warrior Scroll on the right.

05Shield guard behind the planks and Soulstone in the right tank

Continue behind the boards, watching for an ambushing shield soldier.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

A Soulstone is concealed inside the tank on the right.

07Lower elite and cell switch: pass the Nexus and continue straight

Descend the stairs.

Defeat the elite below; it can drop a Crusader Scroll.

Activate the nearby mechanism.

Clear enemies emerging from the cells and take the pictured route.

Descend the stairs to a new Blade Nexus.

Continue straight. The lower right branch is currently a dead end.

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

Break the Soulstone on the left.

09Stairs beyond the zombie platform: enter the giant spider tank room

Clear the undead and collect the white item on the platform.

Watch for an enemy dropping from above at this doorway.

Defeat it and open the chest on the right.

Continue forward and descend these stairs.

A large spider emerges from the right tank below.

Defeat it and collect both red items.

  • The Day the Black Moon Rose I · Location list

The right item is The Day the Black Moon Rose I.

11Lever left of the tank room: ride the elevator to the coffin platform

Another monster emerges from the tank when you take the left item.

Defeat it and collect the red material.

Pull the left lever and take the elevator.

Open the chest behind the coffin above, then dodge its explosion.

12Left path beyond the invisible foe: running jump to the scroll ledge

A short distance ahead, use your lantern to reveal an invisible enemy on the right.

Defeat it and collect the memory ahead.

Turn back. The downward stairs return to the area already explored.

Take the small left path, defeat the undead and ignore the locked left gate.

From the boards, spot a red item below. Use a running jump to reach its platform; walking off misses it.

  • Beast Slaughterer Scroll · Location list

Collect the Beast Slaughterer Scroll.

14Study below the planks: record and jar in the bookshelf corner

Absorb the memory below after taking the scroll.

Enter from the lowest boards. A board breaks beneath you; heal first if low on health to survive the drop.

  • Journey of Life's Creation · Location list

Collect Journey of Life's Creation on the left inside.

Collect the white item on the right.

  • Jarling · Location list

A Jarling hides in the pot by the corner bookshelf.

17Below the study stairs: break the tank for the ghostly swords

Descend the stairs and clear the enemies below.

  • Ghostly Dual Blades · Location list

Break this tank for the Ghostly Dual Blades.

19Opposite laser area: clear the enemies and break the third Soulstone

Cross for the white item. A laser-firing enemy appears here.

  • Soulstone 3 · Location list

Another may attack from behind. A Soulstone lies beside it.

21Left chest and right doorway drop: open the supply route and enter the boss room

After the fight, loot the chest on the left and enter the right door.

Drop to the boss room on the left. The iron gate ahead reconnects to a Nexus if you need to recover.

The boss reuses an earlier enemy's attacks. Apply the strategy for that encounter.

The cinematic confirms that the laboratory's monsters were created by combining humans and animals.
