The Last Guard

01Search for the guard with Tristan: take the stairs left of the broken bridge

Begin The Last Guard side mission.

Speak to Tristan about finding Sivan and Vimon, whose survival could aid Khazan.

Rest. The bridge is blocked, so take the left stairs.

Collect the white item on the main road's right.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

Look across toward the high platform for a Soulstone.

03Mage before the gate and tadpole hall: search right for the farewell letter

Return past the locked left gate and defeat the goblin mage ahead.

Enter and defeat the creature beyond the doorway.

  • Father's Last Letter · Location list

Collect Father's Last Letter on the hall's right.

05Memory by the hall's corpse and Soulstone behind it

Absorb the memory from the corpse ahead, revealing the brothers were infected.

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

Go behind the memory's location for a Soulstone.

07Balcony left of the hall: descend past the elite, then take the spiral stairs

Take the hall's left route.

Collect the golden item on the balcony's right.

Descend the stairs afterward.

Defeat the elite below; it can drop a Pure Guardian Scroll.

08First exit from the spiral stairs: chest and guard record

Climb the spiral stairs on the left.

Take the first exit and open the chest on the right.

  • Bloodstained Guard Record · Location list

Continue into the room and collect the Bloodstained Guard Record on the right.

10Shortcut gate to the hall and the next level up: find the Jarling

The nearby iron gate returns to the earlier hall.

  • Jarling · Location list

Instead, turn back up the spiral staircase and collect the Jarling from the right pot.

12Memory left of the corridor and scroll in the blood pool below

Go left along the corridor ahead for a memory.

Turn back and collect the equipment beside the route.

Continue and drop from this point.

  • Bloodthirsty Fiend Scroll · Location list

Collect the Bloodthirsty Fiend Scroll in the center of the blood pool.

14Drawbridge beside the blood pool and left elevator: face Sivan and Vimon

Go left for a memory and lower the bridge.

Return to rest if needed. From the blood pool, take the left elevator up to the boss room.

Fight the corrupted Sivan and Vimon.

Their memories reveal chaos killed both brothers after the elder infected the younger. This completes the mission.
