Jar Enthusiast

Guide editor: GAME NOTE

GAME NOTE CLASSIC · GUIDE LIBRARYThe First Berserker: Khazan

Accept the jar quest at the hub. Finishing it enables the early chapter Jarling revisits.

01Mission preparations in the Crevice: accept Jar Enthusiast

Speak to Qazumaka downstairs to unlock Blacksmith's Heirloom in the Spider's Tomb.

Beyond the red portal, a new staircase leads to the next major region. Complete the available side missions before proceeding.

Speak to Duimuk at the lower circular platform to accept Jar Enthusiast.

Select the mission through the blue portal.

02Merchant by the bridge and invisible foes: find the first two jars

Speak to Danjin and help find his jars. His shop sells the lantern that reveals invisible enemies.

  • Jarling 1 · Location list

Six Jarlings are hidden here, making loud noises when nearby. The first is before the bridge ahead.

Follow the main path. An invisible enemy waits below, with a memory on the left.

  • Jarling 2 · Location list

The second Jarling is inside the house you descend into.

05Side path outside the house: collect the first Soulstone

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

Leave the house in the pictured direction to find a Soulstone.

07Stairs in the right house: search the houses for the remaining four jars

Climb the ladder inside the right house.

  • Jarling 3 · Location list

The third Jarling is in the house on the left.

  • Jarling 4 · Location list

A short distance ahead, another left-hand house contains the fourth Jarling.

  • Jarling 5 · Location list

After leaving, find the fifth Jarling above on the right.

  • Jarling 6 · Location list

The last Jarling waits at the left end of the road. A return portal appears, but collect the Soulstone on the right before leaving.

12Behind the lookout: collect the Soulstone and return to turn in the mission

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

Break the Soulstone behind the lookout tower.

Back at the hub, Danjin asks you to keep searching. Earlier missions now contain Jarlings; collection milestones unlock his headgear rewards.
