01Meet Andell and Seona: search the flowerbed chest and jar along the left path

Begin the Birth of Evil side mission.

Rest ahead and speak to Andell and Seona nearby.

Continue left after speaking.

Collect the purple item by the flowerbed.

Continue to the chest ahead.

  • Jarling · Location list

After the chest, go a few steps left and break the pots for a Jarling.

03Purple-mist street below the stairs: find the Soulstone on the grass right of the waterwheels

Descend the nearby stairs.

Collect the white item beside the route.

Absorb the memory behind the growth on the right.

Continue toward the two water carts where the main mission had a Soulstone. This mission's stone is nearby but in a different position.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

Find it in the grass on the right.

05Clearing elite and side path: climb the left stairs into the Nexus room

Follow the main road to the elite in the clearing, using the nearby growths against it.

Collect the white item on the right.

Take the red material beside the stairs.

Follow the small path for the main route.

Absorb the roadside memory.

Climb the left stairs and rest inside the house.

06Statue and pillars above the Nexus: gem and Soulstone

  • Ghostly Gem · Location list

Continue upstairs and collect the Ghostly Gem beside the statue.

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

A Soulstone stands behind the pillar.

09Two-level room beyond the door: material below and chest above

Turn back and open this door.

Clear the room and collect the red material behind the downstairs boards.

Climb upstairs and open the chest left of the door.

10Nexus before the boss: clear the ranged foe first, then recover the clown's dagger

Enter for a memory and a Blade Nexus.

The boss room pairs two familiar enemies. Prioritize the ranged opponent.

  • Clown's Dagger · Location list

Victory grants the Clown's Dagger collection item.

The cinematic reveals how Maluka met Gilshan.

Speak to Seona afterward to return.
