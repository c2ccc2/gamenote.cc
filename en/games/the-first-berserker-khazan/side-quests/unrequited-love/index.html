Unrequited Love

Guide editor: GAME NOTE

GAME NOTE CLASSIC · GUIDE LIBRARYThe First Berserker: Khazan

Speak to Tristan and investigate Lydia’s fate.

01Soulstone 1

  • Soulstone 1

Begin Unrequited Love to investigate the death of Tristan's beloved Lydia.

Rest on arrival, then climb the pictured stairs.

Use a throwing attack to bring down the airborne enemy.

A Soulstone hides behind the wooden crates in the corner.

02Soulstone 2

  • Soulstone 2

Explore right of the main staircase.

Collect the material in the corner.

Enter from the main stairs and climb the left staircase inside.

Defeat the soldiers and collect the purple item behind the crates on the right.

Go left and drop here toward more undead.

Collect the nearby items and absorb the memory ahead.

Leave through the large gate, watching for an ambush at the entrance.

Ambush the elite in the hall from behind.

It can drop a Dragon Knight Scroll. Collect the purple item beside its corpse as well.

Open the chest on the left.

From the stairs, look high to the left for a Soulstone.

03Jarling

  • Jarling

Activate this switch to open a connection to the previous area.

Turn back and climb left of the main staircase.

At the end, enter the right door for a memory.

Continue upstairs after collecting it.

Watch for a laser-firing enemy appearing overhead here.

A Jarling hides among the cabinets farther inside.

04Drowned Witch Scroll

  • Drowned Witch Scroll

Open the chest in the small left room.

Leave and follow the left edge to climb this scaffold.

Collect the Drowned Witch Scroll ahead.

05Soulstone 3

  • Soulstone 3

Climb the left stairs to a Blade Nexus.

A ladder descends left of the Nexus. Look up from it for a Soulstone.

06Boss encounter

Rest and absorb the memory on the left.

The boss resembles Maluka's second phase. Use that strategy.

Victory grants equipment and Guido's Worn Diary.

Absorb the memory inside the coffin to trigger the cinematic.

Guido killed his childhood friend Lydia after she rejected him, driven by jealousy of Tristan and already affected by chaos.

Speak to Tristan nearby to return.
