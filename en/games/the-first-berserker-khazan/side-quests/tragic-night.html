01Skeleton village road and bear stairs: reach the Nexus beyond the gate

Accept Tragic Night from Daphrona.

Finish off the skeletons so they cannot revive. The memory on the right is a burrower trap.

Collect the white item beneath the stairs.

Defeat the Powerful Enemy bear above for equipment and items. The memory in front is genuine.

There is no rolling boulder on this slope in the side mission.

Rest at the Nexus beyond the entrance.

02Facing houses beyond the Nexus: diary and Soulstone

  • Boy's Diary · Location list

Collect the Boy's Diary inside the right house.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

The opposite house contains a Soulstone.

05Bridge corner and jar on the left: collect the Fire Summoning Scroll

  • Fire Summon Scroll · Location list

Continue inside for the Fire Summon Scroll in the right corner.

  • Jarling · Location list

Cross the bridge. The memory below is false; collect the Jarling marked on the left.

08Chest in the right wooden house and the Soulstone overhead

Open the chest inside the right wooden house.

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

Turn back and look up for another Soulstone. Destroy it with Javelin.

10End of the left stairs: collect the scroll and face the deer-headed foe again

Climb the stairs and absorb the genuine memory.

  • Fallen Guardian Scroll · Location list

Follow the left path to the red Fallen Guardian Scroll.

  • Happy Boy and Deer · Location list

The deer-headed boss adds an invisible teleport but otherwise resembles Volbaino. Defeat it for equipment and the Happy Boy and Deer collection item.
