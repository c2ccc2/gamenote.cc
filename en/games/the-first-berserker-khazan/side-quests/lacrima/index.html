Lacrima

01Lacrima entrance: clear exploding zombies and cross the bridge for the Soul-Eater Scroll

Speak to Tristan at the hub to unlock Unrequited Love.

More regions and missions appear through the yellow portal. First undertake Lacrima to strengthen your phantom.

Exploding undead wait on arrival. Collect the white item outside the balcony.

Clear the enemies down the stairs and collect the purple item in the middle.

Watch for the dangerous fireball caster on the outside bridge.

  • Soul Eater Scroll · Location list

Defeat it and collect the red Soul Eater Scroll behind it.

03Wooden barrier behind the left door and the lower plank landing

  • Jarling · Location list

Enter the small left doorway. A Jarling hides behind the wooden railing on the left.

Open the chest farther left.

Descend the right-hand stairs afterward.

Absorb the memory in the corner.

A red item is visible below the boards. Choose a safe landing and drop down.

  • Ghostly Sword Hilt · Location list

Collect the Ghostly Sword Hilt.

06Zombies below the pillars: clear fireball enemies and climb to the Soulstone

Descend using the nearby pillar, or jump directly if you can afford the fall damage.

Clear the undead below carefully.

Collect the red material right of the doorway.

Defeat the fire caster ahead, open the corner chest and climb the left stairs.

Two more fire casters wait above. Fight them separately if possible.

Absorb the memory across the bridge.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

Find the Soulstone above the left staircase.

08Stairs on the other side: save before clearing the fire-wielding group

Absorb the memory beside the fire caster on the other side.

Descend this staircase; you climbed the opposite one earlier.

Rest at the Nexus below before the boss.

The fight combines familiar enemies and a fireball caster. Remove the smaller enemies first.

Speak to Blade Phantom afterward to return.
