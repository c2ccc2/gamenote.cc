01Disobedience preparations: search for Yulian's family

Back at the hub, Daphrona's conversation unlocks another area.

You can also accept The Vow during the conversation.

Speak to Yulian to unlock Birth of Evil.

Follow mission levels and complete Disobedience first, helping Yulian find his family.

Speak to Yulian after entering for context.

02Left drop and Boris at the gate: save before exploring the streets

Go left and drop down. Speak to Boris behind the left iron gate about the area's situation.

Rest at the nearby Nexus.

Continue through the monsters, using exploding growths against them.

Climb the right stairs for a memory.

Descend and collect the purple item at the right end.

03Gate with the call for help and lower room: search the chest and climb to the notes

Climb the small stairs on the left.

Follow the route past a white item to an iron gate. Evelyn asks for help from the other side; speak to her.

Drop left afterward and use the growths against the enemies below.

Enter the left room for a blue item on its left side.

Continue inside, first exploring left for a chest before taking the right stairs.

  • Evelyn's Notes · Location list

Return and climb the stairs for Evelyn's Notes straight ahead.

05Drop right of the new Nexus: expose the succubus trap

Absorb the memory ahead on the right.

Turn back toward the small doorway.

Continue to a new Blade Nexus.

Rest, then take the right route past the gate locked from this side and drop here.

Evelyn appears below as a succubus, revealing her earlier plea was a trap.

  • Ghostly Shield · Location list

Defeat her for equipment and collect the red Ghostly Shield beside the street.

07Succubus stairs and shortcut gate: aim at the Soulstone high on the left

Collect the white item on the left.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

Climb the stairs Evelyn descended. Shoot the Soulstone high on the left from the staircase, collect the purple item by the gate and open it from this side.

09Memory in the corpse pile and jar beside the hay

Turn back and continue to the memory among the corpses on the right.

  • Jarling · Location list

The pot in the nearby grass contains a Jarling.

11Boss room through the left doorway: recover the wife's letter and speak to Yulian

Enter the left door for the boss.

This encounter uses another familiar enemy variant.

  • Yulian's Wife's Letter · Location list

Victory grants Yulian's Wife's Letter.

Speak to Yulian to complete the mission. The letter confirms his family is safe.
