The Suicide Squad

Guide editor: GAME NOTE

GAME NOTE CLASSIC · GUIDE LIBRARYThe First Berserker: Khazan

Select this mission after the theatre main quest.

Pickups in this mission

Soulstone locations 1Jarling locations 1Records and story items 4
  1. Soulstone
  2. Jarling, Prophecy III
  3. Prophecy IV
  4. Suicide Squad's Resolve
  5. Ninety-ninth Owl's Last Letter

01Soulstone

  • Soulstone 1

Speak to Tristan automatically after returning to unlock a new area.

Continue speaking to accept Residual Chaos as well.

A new staircase and portal lead to the next main region. Use the left yellow portal for side missions first.

Begin the newly unlocked Suicide Squad mission to investigate the princess's unit and any survivors.

Rest after arrival.

Continue forward through the soldiers.

A Soulstone stands on the bridge's right.

02Jarling, Prophecy III

  • Jarling
  • Prophecy III

Follow the sole route and absorb the corridor memory.

Continue past these stairs to the chest in the corner.

Return and descend the stairs.

First explore right for a white item.

Absorb the memory in the left corner.

Take the left branch, defeat two soldiers and open the hidden mural door.

A Jarling hides in the pot right of the hidden entrance.

Collect Prophecy III farther inside and leave through the left bookshelf passage.

03Prophecy IV

  • Prophecy IV

Go left outside to another doorway.

Descend into the corridor and open the hidden mural door on the left.

Collect Prophecy IV on the left inside.

04Suicide Squad's Resolve

  • Suicide Squad's Resolve

Open the chest on the right, then return to the corridor.

Collect the white item straight ahead.

Continue to the Blade Nexus.

Collect the Suicide Squad's Resolve at the boss entrance.

05Ninety-ninth Owl's Last Letter

  • Ninety-ninth Owl's Last Letter

Defeat the corrupted soldiers and familiar enemy variants beyond the door.

Collect the Ninety-ninth Owl's Last Letter afterward and return.
