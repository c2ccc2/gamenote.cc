01Entrance unlocked by three fragments: open the gate and ride down to the Nexus

The Vow is a hidden mission unlocked by collecting all three disc fragments. Their positions are listed in the collection guide.

Open the iron gate directly ahead after arrival.

Continue to the elevator.

Rest at the new Nexus below.

02Hidden stone wall behind the statue: search the chest and descend through the side doorway

Absorb the memory behind the statue.

Break the stone wall behind it to reveal a hidden doorway.

Open the chest inside.

Leave and take the small side door.

Collect the blue item beside the route.

Continue inside and descend the stairs.

03Triggered trap and collapsing floor: defeat the elite below

Avoid the proximity trap on the lower path.

The floor ahead collapses into an elite encounter. Prepare before dropping.

Defeat the elite below and collect the nearby items.

  • Ghostly Spear Blade · Location list

The red item in the right corner is the Ghostly Spear Blade.

05Gate beyond the elite room: search the hidden wall on the right

Collect the purple item beside the elite's position.

Leave through the nearby iron gate.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

The right branch is a dead end, but its hidden wall conceals a Soulstone.

07Stairs in the left fork's room: pass the hidden door to the relic chest

Turn back to the fork and explore left first.

In the small left room, break the hidden wall beneath the stairs.

Watch for the trap beyond the doorway; it may trigger without stepping directly on its plate.

  • Ghostly Relic · Location list

The room's chest contains a Ghostly Relic.

09Return to the statue room on the right: climb and search right for the jar first

Return and take the previous fork's right branch.

Collect the red material in front of the statue.

Climb the stairs.

At the upper fork, explore right first.

Collect the purple item in the right corner.

  • Jarling · Location list

The pot on the left contains a Jarling.

11Other skeleton corridor: collect Netherworld Energy before dropping down

The stairs here return to the earlier collapsed-floor area. Turn back and take the other path.

  • Netherworld Energy · Location list

Clear the large skeletons in that corridor and collect the golden Netherworld Energy upgrade behind you.

13Lower cells and trapped doorway: find the subjugation record behind the elevator

Drop from above afterward.

Absorb the memory directly below.

Collect the blue item in the nearby open cell.

Enter this doorway carefully past the trap ahead.

Clear the skeleton soldiers farther inside.

Open the iron gate after the fight.

  • Hismar Campaign III · Location list

Before taking the elevator ahead, turn back for Hismar Campaign III.

15Nexus below the elevator: search the left waterfall at the fork first

Take the elevator down to a new Nexus.

Continue down the stairs for a purple item on the right.

The memory farther ahead conceals a burrower. Strike first.

The main route goes right. Explore left first for a blue item on the rock.

  • Hismar Campaign IV · Location list

Collect Hismar Campaign IV beneath the small waterfall on the left.

17Main route by the right pool: drop down and face the gatekeeper

Explore right to the chest at the end.

Return to the main route and collect the blue item on the rock in the right pool.

Another blue item lies on the rock ahead.

The next memory is also a burrower trap.

Drop from this position.

Absorb the memory beside the route.

Continue toward the gatekeeper and attack from range before closing in.

It can drop a Raider Scroll.

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

A Soulstone stands behind the pillar on the right.

19Nexus beyond the gate: hidden door on the left and duplicating boss

Enter the large gate.

Absorb the memory on the left and reach the Nexus ahead.

Open the hidden door on the left.

Loot the chest inside.

Rest and enter the large gate to fight a familiar duplicating enemy.

20Chapel after the battle: find Reese's Ring and read the notebook

After victory, enter the rear chapel and collect Reese's Ring.

Interact with the notebook beside it to trigger a cinematic.

The memory follows Reese and Ozma. Despite Hismar's defeat, evil remained, and Ozma wanted to eliminate it entirely.

On the intended wedding day, the emperor demanded Reese become his consort. Her attempt to flee failed.

She could now trust only Plebus. This part of the memory ends here.
