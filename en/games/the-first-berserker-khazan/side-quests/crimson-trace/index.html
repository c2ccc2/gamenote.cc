Crimson Trace

01Crimson Trace entrance: search the right steps, then the left corridor

Speak to Tristan at the hub to unlock The Last Guard.

Crimson Trace also becomes available. Complete it first.

Collect the purple item on the right steps after arrival.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

Take the left route to the Soulstone at the corridor's end.

03Steps and Prophecy beyond the duplicating elite

Resume the main route and fight the elite whose duplicates attack alongside it.

Collect the golden material on the left steps after victory.

  • Prophecy II · Location list

Continue to Prophecy II on the left side of the route.

05Memory ahead and high left ledge: find the Soulstone, then climb for the jar

Collect the memory ahead and defeat the familiar enemy.

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

A Soulstone sits high on the left.

Continue and climb the left ladder.

Absorb the memory on the room's floor.

  • Jarling · Location list

The pot beside it contains a Jarling.

08Nexus at the end and paired elites: meet Lantimos after the fight

Follow the main route and collect the equipment in the middle.

Rest at the Nexus ahead before entering the boss room.

Defeat the two elite enemies in this encounter.

Speak to Lantimos, who reveals Skalpel often disappeared through the room below.

09Lower bookshelf passage: open the chest for the Singing Eagle's Hair

Descend and open the hidden bookshelf passage.

  • Singing Hawk's Hair · Location list

The chest below contains the Singing Hawk's Hair. Collect it and return.
