Pavel’s Final Words

Guide editor: GAME NOTE

Select the mission after Devoured Village.

01Return to the old town with Tristan: search the right corner

Back at the hub, speaking to Tristan unlocks Linon Mine.

First complete the Pavel's Final Words side mission.

Rest on arrival, turn right and speak to Tristan.

Advance and turn right. Enemies release pursuing skulls when killed; dodge those as well.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

Continue straight for a Soulstone in the corner.

03Raised area left of the gate: clear ranged foes before climbing to the Nexus

Return from the Soulstone and go left of this doorway.

Several enemies guard the area. Use Javelin against those on raised platforms first.

Collect the white item in the right recess after the fight.

Climb the ladder for a purple item on the platform.

Rest at the Nexus above.

04Houses beyond the elite guard: chest on the right and jar on the left

An elite waits deeper inside.

After defeating it, check the right dead end for a chest.

  • Jarling · Location list

The first house on the left contains a Jarling.

06Second house and corridor ladder: collect the recipe and search upstairs

  • Assassin Recipe · Location list

Collect the Assassin Recipe inside the second house.

Absorb the corridor memory and open the left gate, then climb the right stairs.

08Upper fork: search right before exploring deep into the left branch

Both directions are accessible above.

Explore right first, defeating enemies for a white item at the dead end, then return left.

Fight through the enemies to the chest farther left.

  • Angry Bandit Scroll · Location list

Collect the Angry Bandit Scroll beside the corpse in the corner.

10Elite beneath the stairs and elevator switch: orders and Soulstone

  • Bandit's Orders · Location list

Defeat the elite beneath the stairs for the Bandit's Orders.

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

Activate the elevator switch. While waiting, turn around and destroy the Soulstone above, then board.

13Save at the elevator landing: challenge the Rangkus-like foe

Rest at the new Nexus below.

Absorb the memory ahead.

Enter the boss room. This enemy reuses Rangkus's moves, so apply that strategy.
