01Residual Chaos entrance: turn back for the memory and left Soulstone

Next complete Residual Chaos, the final side mission in this region.

Rest on arrival, then turn around and absorb the memory on the ground.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

Enter from the left to find a Soulstone in the corner.

03Inside the purple portal: clear the enemy waves to activate the switch

Return to the main route and enter the purple portal.

Collect the white item in the right corner after teleporting.

Absorb the memory in the middle of the route.

Collect the purple item beside the first light gate on the left.

Clear the many waves emerging from the portals. Spawning stops after the final elite falls.

Only then can you operate the mechanism ahead.

04Return through the portal: search the left chest and Elamein's notes on the main path

Activate it and return through the portal.

Open the chest on the left after teleporting.

  • Elamein's Notes · Location list

Follow the main route beside it and collect Elamein's Notes.

06Switch at the main path's end: teleport again to the experimental record

Absorb the memory in the corner ahead.

Continue to the end and activate the switch.

Use the portal after activating it.

  • Tania's Experiment Log III · Location list

Collect Tania's Experiment Log III directly ahead.

08Hidden door left of the stairs: scroll and corner jar

Follow the main route upstairs and open the hidden door on the left.

  • Endless Tragedy Scroll · Location list

Collect the red Endless Tragedy Scroll inside.

  • Jarling · Location list

A Jarling hides in the corner pot.

11Tanya beyond the Nexus: trigger Elamein's memory after the battle

Leave and rest at the Nexus ahead before the boss.

Tania resembles a familiar lesser enemy and steals health on hit. Maintain enough damage to overcome her healing.

Victory triggers a memory involving Elamein and ends the mission.
