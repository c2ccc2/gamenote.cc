Boss rewards and farming

Guide editor: GAME NOTE

GAME NOTE CLASSIC · GUIDE LIBRARYThe First Berserker: Khazan

Since June 16, 2025, main-mission bosses guarantee set-crafting materials. This does not guarantee a specific finished item, full set or scroll.

Before farming

  • Choose a set, then check the recipe and missing materials at the blacksmith. Missing a recipe is different from missing a boss material.
  • Return through the mission map to replay the relevant mission. Do not assume resting at a cleared boss checkpoint respawns that boss.
  • The table covers sixteen main bosses and explicitly recorded rewards. Potential sets are not exclusive sources, and named gear pieces are not guaranteed.
