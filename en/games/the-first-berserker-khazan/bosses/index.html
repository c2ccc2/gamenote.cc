Blade Phantom (boss)

Stormpass boss; read attack strings by weapon and phase.

  • A spear throw can lead into a teleporting slam; hammer attacks create frontal spikes.
  • Phase two adds claws and teleports; respond to Burst Attacks with Counterattack.

Viper

Forgotten Temple boss; read spear and transformed fist/dagger phases separately.

  • Avoid guarding too early against delayed spear attacks; move away from grabs.
  • Distinguish fast daggers from slower punches, and watch for rock throws after palm slams.
