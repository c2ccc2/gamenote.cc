Encounter walkthrough

The memory ahead conceals another burrower.

Climb the left ladder for a chest.

Continue up the stairs and follow the route to a Blade Nexus.

Rest before the boss, a variant of Yetuga.

Speak to the two people behind the arena after victory to finish the mission.
