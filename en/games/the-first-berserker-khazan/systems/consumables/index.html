Consumables: cleansing, resistance and preparation

Guide editor: GAME NOTE

Prepare four roles

  • Separate health recovery, combat-resource recovery, relevant cleanses and resistance or enhancement items. Put what this mission needs on shortcuts.

  • After buying or crafting, check shortcuts and inventory. Shortcut refilling at a Nexus does not generate unlimited consumables; distinguish stored items from charged healing supplies.

Recovery and resources

  • Willpower Orb spends Spirit; decide whether that leaves enough for your defensive or offensive skill.

  • Recovery actions need safe openings. Being far from a boss is not safe if a projectile sequence is still active.

Cleansing versus resistance

  • A cleanse treats an active ailment; resistance items and accessories address the corresponding threat. Similar names do not make them interchangeable.

  • Identify the ailment before using an item. Even Special Cleanse is not a promise to remove every environmental or story restriction.

Enhancements and throwables

  • Fire, Water, Lightning, Earth and Poison enhancers apply a matching weapon attribute. Spirit fuels and concentrated essences are thrown items, not the same weapon enchantment.

  • Check Daphrona’s Codex before choosing an element. Do not spend materials based solely on color against a resistant or immune target.

  • Cleanses, resistance and enhancements have separate roles and do not remove the need to evade or counter dangerous attacks.

Crafting and recipe routes

  • After unlocking the blacksmith, select the consumables category. Transmutation Shards are generic consumable materials; Spirit Orbs support elemental items, Inner Essence supports combat resources, and Transmutation Mist supports special recipes.

  • Recipes come from pickups and Codex challenges. If crafting is unavailable, inspect the recipe before materials and Gold rather than assuming every item is craftable from the start.

Exploration and recovery tools

  • Returning to a Nexus, leaving a mission, recovering lost Lacrima and resetting attributes are different actions. Read the confirmation before using an item.

  • For the lantern’s purchase and use, consult Danjin and Jar Enthusiast.

Boss-oriented shortcuts

  • For Aratra, prepare healing, poison cleansing and resistance. For Rangkus, consider fire threats; Shactuka, plague; Ozma, chaos and endurance across a longer fight.

  • Keep a small familiar selection on shortcuts. After a failed attempt, distinguish missing cleansing, resistance, stamina and a safe item-use window.
