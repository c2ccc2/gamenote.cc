Combat basics: defense, evasion and resources

Stamina, Spirit and health

  • Stamina supports ordinary attacks, guards and dodges. Long strings, repeated rolls or blocking an entire combo can leave too little for the next response. Start with a short sequence you can stop safely.

  • Spirit is a separate resource for the Javelin and Spirit-consuming skills. Build it through combat; extra Spirit from particular defensive actions depends on learned skills or equipment.

  • A Spirit-consuming skill does not guarantee that every part of its effect is stamina-free. A skill that triggers guards can still involve stamina costs. Read its tooltip and current balance notes.

  • Health measures damage you can survive; stamina measures actions you can sustain. If you repeatedly become exhausted with health remaining, review action costs before adding damage.

Defensive options at a glance

  • Button assignments vary by platform and custom controls. Identify Guard, Dodge, Counterattack and Reflection in the controls menu before practising their timing.

  • Ordinary guard can help you observe a blockable string, but do not hold it indefinitely. Check stamina after each exchange, including elemental damage and ailment buildup.

  • Brink Guard is a guard timed just before impact. It reduces stamina loss relative to ordinary guarding and damages enemy stamina; it does not block every move or make defense universally free.

  • Brink Dodge is a well-timed dodge that briefly enhances dodge attacks. The resulting opening still determines whether a follow-up is safe.

Burst Attacks, grabs and Counterattack

  • A Burst warning gives you time to prepare. Watch the actual incoming move rather than countering the moment the icon, sound or red flash appears.

  • Ordinary guard and Brink Guard do not stop Burst Attacks. Evade their path or time the unlocked Counterattack action at impact. A successful Burst counter restores stamina and creates an opening.

  • Do not assume grabs can be guarded. Move out of reach or dodge, and do not treat every special visual effect as the same counterable attack type.

  • Countering an ordinary attack does not promise the same recovery or interruption as a Burst counter. Beginners should first practise against clear Burst warnings.

Reflection is not Brink Guard

  • Reflection is an active skill with unlock requirements. Its interception animation must meet the incoming attack; that differs from simply timing a guard before impact.

  • Practise on a familiar slower move before attempting it against a new multi-hit string. Failure can cost health and stamina, so keep using the other defensive options.

  • Weapon-specific skills such as Greatsword’s Counterstrike have their own costs and conditions. Do not treat similarly named actions as interchangeable.

Stamina damage, exhaustion and Brutal Attacks

  • Health damage is only part of an exchange. Attacks and effective defense can wear down enemy stamina toward exhaustion; running out yourself can leave you unable to act.

  • When the red Brutal Attack prompt appears, position correctly and act before its window ends. Extra normal hits must leave enough time to perform the finisher.

  • A Brutal Attack damages the target and restores stamina. Secure the finisher first, then practise adding damage before it.

  • Exhaustion windows vary by enemy and version. The first three main bosses received duration changes in June 2025; do not use one fixed timer for every boss.

A practical learning order

  • First, use one weapon and a short string against ordinary enemies. Check the stamina left when you stop.

  • Second, learn a slower blockable string and its final hit before practising Brink Guard. Change one timing at a time.

  • Third, dodge when you need to leave an attack’s range or avoid a poor guarding matchup. Do not ignore grabs or elemental threats to force an all-guard approach.

  • Fourth, practise Burst counters at clear teaching encounters such as the Chapter 2 bear. Read the warning, then the path and impact.

  • Fifth, secure Brutal Attacks consistently before extending strings with Spirit skills and Reflection.

Troubleshooting failed attempts

  • Always out of stamina: shorten strings, reduce aimless rolls and check weight and learned skills instead of adding health alone.

  • Damaged while guarding: identify a Burst Attack, grab, elemental effect or guard break before choosing a different response.

  • Punished while healing: wait out the full projectile sequence or combo, then heal during recovery. Distance alone is not a safe window.

  • Unused Spirit: choose one reliable skill and test it in a clear opening while retaining a defensive exit.

  • Correct buttons but failed counters: distinguish Counterattack, Reflection, Brink Guard and weapon skills, then check unlocks and animation timing.
