Set crafting: special materials and scroll routes

Four checks before crafting

  • Unlock crafting through Blacksmith’s Heirloom and register the corresponding scroll.

  • Select the individual slot and inspect special materials, generic materials and Gold. Recheck when slot, level or grade changes.

  • Since June 16, 2025, main bosses guarantee set-crafting materials. This does not guarantee a particular finished piece, complete set or scroll.

  • Material regions and scroll locations are separate. Follow the scroll route for a missing recipe and the relevant area or boss for a missing material.

Generic materials

  • Weapons, armor and accessories use different generic material families such as blades, fragments and stones, with grade-dependent requirements.

  • Inspect the missing ingredients at the blacksmith before dismantling unwanted gear. Distinguish special-material counts, set thresholds and individual recipe costs.

  • Continue available missions while resources are short. Farm repeatedly after choosing the exact item and effect you need.
