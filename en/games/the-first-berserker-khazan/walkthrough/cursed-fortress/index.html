01Attendant Lantimos's Diary

  • Attendant Lantimos's Diary

Resume the main story in Cursed Fortress to find Skalpel.

Follow the only route and climb the stairs.

Speak to Tristan above.

Collect the purple item left of the doorway before leaving.

Choose your landing carefully and drop at the entrance.

Follow the exterior path to a Blade Nexus.

Interact with the bookshelf to reveal a passage.

Descend inside for Attendant Lantimos's Diary.

02Ghostly Shield

  • Ghostly Shield

Continue forward, watching for floor traps beneath the stairs.

Go right below, anticipating an ambush from the right cell.

Remove the archer ahead from range.

Time your crossing past the swinging iron ball.

Drop on the left after crossing.

The left door cannot be opened from here. Go right.

Another swinging ball guards the corridor. First enter the small left door.

Defeat two skeletons and collect the white item in the dead end.

Continue and climb the stairs.

Explore right for a purple item while avoiding the iron ball.

Return left and lure the charging shield soldier into the swinging ball if possible.

Turn left here, timing the iron ball at the intersection.

Another shield soldier waits left. Avoid being knocked off.

At the next fork, descend left first.

At the bottom, turn left at the end for a white item.

Defeat the elite inside the right room.

Open the iron gate on the left afterward.

Break the left boards for a chest.

Collect the Ghostly Shield from the chest.

03Soulstone 1

  • Soulstone 1

A different cell contains a white item. The remaining empty cell need not be opened.

Return to the main route.

Eliminate the next archer before crossing the swinging ball. The cage on the right contains a white item and offers shelter.

Watch for the left enemy's ambush and avoid the floor trigger ahead.

Collect the item on the ground.

First take the left branch down the stairs.

Absorb the memory below and open the right gate as a shortcut to the earlier area.

Climb back and go right this time.

Defeat the corridor skeleton and break the hidden wall on the left.

Break the Soulstone inside.

04Jarling

  • Jarling

Continue upward through a narrow passage containing a shield soldier and a trap.

Climb the left stairs beyond it.

Activate the switch to open the iron gate.

Rest at the new Nexus beyond the gate.

Collect the items in front of it.

Turn back and enter the route left of the stairs.

Clear the soldiers and climb the left staircase.

Open the exploding chest on the left.

Turn back and absorb the memory behind the tent.

Climb the right stairs. Enter directly or circle around the left side.

Collect the blue item outside.

Inside, climb the left stairs. The right room has only optional white items.

Watch for archers igniting the explosive barrels above.

Choose a landing and drop through the central hole onto the boards.

The boards hold this mission's Jarling.

05Soulstone 2

  • Soulstone 2

Drop to the earlier right doorway and climb the stairs again.

This time go right of the opening. A proximity-triggered trap guards the route; dodge through as you approach.

Collect the blue item on the mandatory route.

Climb the ladder into another corridor with enemies and explosive barrels.

Outside ahead, collect the purple item on the right beam.

Climb the left staircase.

Open the iron gate and enter.

Climb the long ladder inside.

Clear the enemies above and absorb the memory on the right.

Follow the left edge and drop for a purple item.

Descend through the window.

Continue outside the castle and move quickly as the right stone wall breaks open.

Open the chest on the right inside the breach.

Leave and continue along the boards for a purple item around the bend.

Climb to the Blade Nexus and rest.

Collect the white item on the rooftop's right side.

Keep moving under catapult fire. Follow the sole route and collect the blue item in the corner.

Continue for a memory in the right corner.

Drop from the pictured position afterward.

Climb this ladder below.

Drop through the central hole above.

Look left after landing for the Soulstone behind the crates.

06Soulstone 3

  • Soulstone 3

Follow this route and turn back on the right for a blue item.

A ladder on the left leads to a gate reconnecting with the previous area.

Collect the golden material in the room.

Return to the main route and open the chest right of the door.

Enter and descend.

The room has little loot. Outside right, two enemies guard no collection items.

Absorb the memory in the left corner.

Turn back and continue straight toward an elite shield soldier.

Defeat it and collect the blue item on the right.

Turn left along this route.

Climb the stairs and go onto the balcony for a Soulstone.

07Prophecy I

  • Prophecy I

Return and take the pictured route.

Collect Prophecy I inside.

08Soulstone 4

  • Soulstone 4
  • Great General Scroll

Return to the main route and descend these stairs.

Inside the castle, pull this switch to open the left iron gate.

Descend beyond it and go left on the level ground for a red material.

Pass through the doorway for a chest behind it.

Descend the right stairs afterward.

Activate the lower mechanism to open the iron gate.

The gate reconnects with the previous Nexus.

Rest, then enter the large gate directly.

Absorb the memory beside the stairs.

At the first doorway ahead, take the right stairs.

Defeat the two soldiers for the Great General Scroll in the corner.

Return to the main road and collect the white item on the left.

At this doorway, climb the left stairs.

Continue inside, defeat the soldiers and break the Soulstone behind them.

09Vaisar Disc Fragment

  • Vaisar Disc Fragment
  • Worn Diary

Return to the main route and destroy the machine firing arrows ahead.

Collect the white item on the right platform.

Avoid lingering in the blood pool, which inflicts plague. Collect the Worn Diary in the left corner.

Defeat the enemy on the right and take the right stairs.

Open the chest inside.

Climb the forward stairs to absorb a memory.

Finally, take the elevator on the left.

Rest at the new Nexus above.

Climb into the hall and collect the purple Vaisar Disc Fragment on the left.

10Soulstone 5

  • Soulstone 5

Break the hidden wall on the right for another Soulstone.

The large gate on the main route leads to the boss.

11Boss encounter: Skalpel

Enter to find Skalpel wielding a greatsword and carrying a coffin, already corrupted by chaos.

Skalpel has two phases.

Attack ①:

A three-slash combo with a delayed final hit.

Attack ②:

Another three-slash combo ends in a Burst Attack.

Attack ③:

A shockwave precedes his climb onto the coffin, which he rides toward you through four waves.

Attack ④:

An attack from behind the coffin vehicle causes a relatively small explosion around it.

Attack ⑤:

A jumping coffin shockwave. Time your response to the landing.

Attack ⑥:

The coffin glows before a grab pulls you inside. Listen for the audio cue as well.

After the first health bar, he enters the coffin. The mission-complete message does not mean the fight is over.

He emerges again, seemingly controlled by the coffin. During phase two, he slowly loses health on his own.

Phase two — Attack ①:

Three ranged shockwaves. Dodge with each downward strike.

Attack ②:

He jumps, launches tentacles from the coffin and slams down, then charges an explosion around himself.

Attack ③:

Short-range homing projectiles emerge around the sword. Back away and they hit the ground harmlessly.

Attack ④:

Another tentacle sequence begins on the ground, then repeats twice with jumps.

Other moves resemble phase one. Defeat him again to win.

His memory reveals he resisted Ozma and continued believing in Khazan's innocence.

Blade Phantom protects Khazan from chaos afterward. Both now recognize Ozma as their shared enemy.

Khazan prepares to confront the master of chaos, ending the chapter.
