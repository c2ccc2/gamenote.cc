Village and storehouse route

Descend from Cabin on the Hill. Watch the buried Crypt Devourer outside the cave: its grab can throw you off the cliff.

Continue to Phraugh Village Dock, open the large door near its shortcut and advance toward the storehouse.

Drop into the village branch from Central Storehouse Entrance, inspect the ladder building and elite room, then return to open the boss approach.

Route illustration

Soulstones and story records

  • First Soulstone: follow the cave’s right wall to a ladder and climb it.
  • Second: immediately left after opening the large door near the dock shortcut.
  • Third: above the wooden stairs opposite the elite-room entrance; check its left side for the Ashen Wolf Scroll.
  • Evacuation Order: enter the open building after the storehouse drop, climb its ladder, drop opposite and check the exit.

Exploration illustration

Jarling location

After Jar Enthusiasts, drop from Central Storehouse Entrance and check the jar immediately right.

Volbaino: battle notes

  • Check Necklace of Sealed Fire and Fire Cleansing Potions before the fight. Punish completed strings with short attacks and reserve stamina.
  • A planted spike can lead to a hammer shockwave: move away. Later, pulling the weapon free leaves persistent fire.
  • Use the ladder to escape lower-floor ignition. Avoid standing between the boss and a thrown spike.

Later mission illustration

After the battle return notes

Back in The Crevice, speak to Duimuk about Jar Enthusiasts. After completing it, return to Central Storehouse Entrance, drop down and check the small pot on the right before continuing to mission five.
