01Magic orbs outside the palace: follow the stairs to Danjin's Nexus

After Reese, the story sends you directly to the Imperial Palace.

Advance while dodging the magic orbs attacking from above.

Follow the sole path ahead.

Defeat the skeletons inside and absorb the memory at the end.

Take the main route right of the memory and climb the stairs.

First explore left above for a purple item.

Return and climb the main slope.

Drop down to the Blade Nexus.

Speak to Danjin right of the Nexus for three chaos-cleansing consumables.

02Moving stone passage: clear the elite beyond the gate and ride the elevator up

Continue and ride the moving stone blocks across.

An elite emerges when you open the opposite door.

Defeat it and continue inside for a chest on the left.

Pull the lever to call the elevator.

Ride up to a Nexus at the doorway.

03Gap beside the Nexus: search the lower chest and climb back

Rest, then drop through the nearby gap.

Enter this small doorway.

Open the chest inside.

Return to the drop's landing area and climb the ladder there.

04Branches beside the statue: search supplies and memories before the main door

Follow the pictured direction above.

Collect the white item by the left statue. Leave the door opposite it for later.

Continue and descend the next staircase for another white item.

Return up and take the left path.

Climb the stairs for a memory.

Return to the large door you passed earlier and enter.

Absorb the memory immediately inside.

Climb the nearby stairs. The right side has an openable door; the left is blocked.

05Outdoor pavilions and magic orbs: climb the platforms to the next Nexus

Follow the main route outside, where magic orbs resume attacking.

Descend the left stairs for a purple item in the pavilion.

Climb back up for a white item in the upper pavilion.

Drop one level beside it for another white item on the right.

Return to the main route.

Defeat the three skeleton soldiers at this position.

Absorb the memory on the right afterward.

Climb the nearby ladder.

Drop in the pictured direction above.

Rest at the new Nexus ahead.

06Elite hall beyond the gate: search both upper sides and pull the lever to open the tunnel

Collect the white item behind the right statue.

Enter the large gate toward another elite.

First climb the right stairs for a purple item.

Cross to the far end opposite for another purple item.

Return to defeat the hall's elite, then climb the stairs and pull the switch.

07Soulstone left of the tunnel and return ladder on the right

A passage opens below.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

Descend into it and go left first for a Soulstone.

09Upper gap and beams: collect the memory, then cross the crumbling outdoor stones

Return and climb the right-hand ladder.

Drop through the gap on the left above for a memory.

This returns you to the hall. Reenter the lower passage and climb back to the gap.

Cross the beam this time, watching for interference from enemies.

Collect the purple item along the beam.

Continue outside.

The small stone blocks ahead collapse. Cross without lingering.

Collect the white item on the route.

10Descend to the balcony: rotate the stairs with the switch and search the corridor Soulstone

Drop from this position.

Continue descending to a balcony.

Step on the trigger to rotate the staircase, then turn back into the corridor first.

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

A Soulstone waits at the corridor's end.

12Rotate the stairs twice: reach Daphrona's ending-choice Nexus

Return up the rotating stairs for the memory outside the balcony.

Descend and step on the trigger again, then climb the stairs to access a different route.

Explore left ahead for a blue item.

Return right and rest at the new Nexus.

Speak to Daphrona for the ending choice. The left and right options lead to different branches, while the bottom option leads toward the truth ending. The right branch adds a battle.

The truth route requires all 15 diary items, including The Day the Black Moon Rose III from Transcendental Sword. With the requirements met, choose the bottom dialogue option.

13Study beyond the Nexus: search the right bookshelf and cross the central beams

Follow the pictured direction for a memory.

Climb these stairs afterward.

In the study above, first clear the goblins on the right and operate the bookshelf.

Collect the golden material inside.

Turn back and cross this shelf.

Collect the red material in the right corner.

A white item lies on the shelf across the way.

14Bookshelf switch beside the beams: climb through the passage to the overhead Soulstone

Stop on the central shelf and inspect the mechanism on the right bookshelf.

The main route lies behind the bookshelf.

Absorb the memory on the left inside.

Go right and climb the stairs afterward.

  • Soulstone 3 · Location list

In the next corridor, look up for a Soulstone.

16Spiral stairs and outer balcony: circle to the room's Nexus

At the end, climb another spiral staircase.

Defeat the laser-firing enemy on the stairs and continue straight.

The next stairs lead both up and down.

Explore downstairs for a blue item, then climb.

Collect the blue item on the left near the upper exit.

Leave and circle around the outside balcony.

Continue upward, watching for an enemy dropping at the doorway.

Open the chest on the room's right.

Leave left for a new Blade Nexus.

17Rooms beyond the Nexus: elite chest on the left and jar on the right

Rest and continue inward. Defeat the elite in the left building for materials and a chest.

Return to the main route and ignore the left door. Take the right stairs.

Climb the right-hand staircase first.

  • Jarling · Location list

A Jarling hides behind the fallen cabinet in the right corridor.

19Opening in the left room and barrel corridor: search the memory and explosive chest

Collect the purple item through the left door.

Drop through the room's hole and leave through this doorway.

Descend the stairs here.

Collect the red material in the left corner below.

Absorb the memory behind the barrels on the right main route.

Climb the left stairs for an exploding chest.

20Hidden wall in the room up the right slope: break the final Soulstone

Return below and climb the right slope.

  • Soulstone 4 · Location list

Break the hidden wall in the room above for a Soulstone.

22Balcony elevator mechanism: raise the platform for material, then descend and leave

Continue inward. This door reconnects with the earlier route but need not be opened.

A red item is visible on the right balcony but inaccessible. Descend the spiral stairs and activate the lift first.

Return upstairs to collect the material.

Go back to the first floor, activate the lift and head outside.

Collect the red item beneath the stairs.

23Courtyard stairs and central drop: search the material and reach the Nexus

Continue down the main route.

Climb the steps for a blue item on the left.

A red item in the right atrium cannot be reached directly.

Take the stairs behind you.

Absorb the memory upstairs.

Drop through the central atrium afterward.

Collect the red material and leave by the side passage.

Continue inward.

Rest at the Nexus ahead.

24Supplies beside the Nexus: search the succubus chest, then enter the right elevator

Explore this side for loot after resting.

Collect the red material inside.

Go to the other side and ambush the succubus visible within.

Open the chest beside her.

Return to the main route afterward.

Enter and turn right.

Take the elevator inside.

25Tilting stone slabs: cross the platforms and wait for the rising block

Continue ahead above toward more moving stone platforms.

Ride the stones to the opposite platform.

Collect the purple item beside the route.

On the next slab, quickly reach the marked position before it tilts and throws you off.

Follow the edge afterward.

Climb the stairs and go left.

Descend here without lingering on the collapsing slabs.

Collect the purple item along the way.

Climb the stairs and continue along the small path.

Again, hurry to the marked position before the slab flips.

Follow the side for a purple item ahead.

After collecting it, wait in place for the stone slab to descend on the right.

26Opening beside the upper elevator: drop to the main path and cross the skeleton platforms

Ride it upward. The left door is locked, so continue up the stairs.

The elevator above returns to an earlier Nexus if you need to rest.

The main route drops through the hole beside the elevator.

Continue straight after landing.

Collect the purple item on the left platform.

Absorb the memory in the middle of the path.

Absorb another memory on the right platform.

Continue toward the skeleton shield soldiers.

27Spiral stairs on the right: open the gate to the final Nexus

Defeat them and climb the right spiral staircase.

Clear the doorway and open the large left gate.

Rest at the Nexus beyond it.

28Before Ozma's gate: final conversation and battle preparations

Climb after resting. Ozma awaits beyond the next gate.

Ozma initially looks unchanged, but now believes humanity itself is the world's problem.

They saved the world only to be abandoned, and Reese was killed.

Ozma asks Khazan to join him.

Khazan rejects him, remembering his actions and seeking justice for Reese and their companions.

The final boss fight begins as Ozma unleashes chaos.

29Lord of Chaos phase one: sword attacks and elemental spells

Ozma has two main phases; the earlier right-hand ending branch adds a third duel.

His principal attacks are listed below.

Attack ①:

Two slashes lead into a charged weapon plunge and a large explosion around him. Time your dodge for the blast.

Attack ②:

A charged forward shockwave with a clear release timing.

Attack ③:

Arcane projectiles that can be evaded.

Attack ④:

He rises and throws down a charged Javelin, resembling your own spear attack.

Attack ⑤:

An aerial fire column strikes forward and explodes a second time.

Attack ⑥:

Forward ice spikes travel with a similar speed and reach to the fire column.

Attack ⑦:

A wide attack first pulls you toward him without damage, then charges a large blast. Walk clear during the long wind-up.

These cover the main first-phase attacks.

30Ozma's second form: combos, slams and area blasts

Depleting the first health bar triggers the second form.

Attack ①:

Four basic attacks followed by a potent strike that can be deflected.

Attack ②:

A shockwave with a very obvious charge. Dodge at release.

Attack ③: healing crystal. Ozma summons a purple crystal in front of him and prepares to absorb it to restore health. Switch attention to the crystal and destroy it rather than attacking only the boss. The crystal emits area pulses: dodge the outward energy wave, then resume attacking it instead of committing through the pulse.

Supplemental reference ↗

Attack ④:

Three jumping slams drop large spheres nearby. The first two are quicker; the third has a longer charge and ends in a surrounding explosion. Retreat early.

Attack ⑤:

He flies up, fires projectiles and finishes with an explosive slam. Stay clear of the landing.

Attack ⑥:

A close-range grab. Watch the charging hand.

31After the final battle: scroll reward and ending branches

  • Lord of Chaos Scroll · Location list

Defeating the second phase grants the Lord of Chaos Scroll.

If you chose the earlier right-hand ending branch, complete a third duel as well.

The illustrated left-branch ending shows Khazan killing Ozma.

33Ozma's residence: explore the memories and lay a flower at the portrait

The credits follow.

Khazan revisits a place filled with memories.

In Ozma's residence, first interact with the object on the right for a memory.

Inspect the object on the table next.

Descend and absorb the memory to trigger a cinematic.

It shows Reese's death and the circumstances of Ozma's fall after the treason accusation.

Climb the left stairs after the lower memory.

Inspect the portrait and offer a flower to reach the ending.

This concludes the campaign walkthrough.

You can begin New Game+ after completing the campaign.
