Palace and rotation mechanism

Open the hidden stairs from Cathedral Entrance, inspect the lower-left branch and reach the pressure-plate room.

Rotate the room, exit through the entrance door and check the revealed path. At Corrupted Lounge, review ending requirements before Daphrona’s dialogue.

Continue through the curved balcony, palace garden and basement; replenish recovery before the final arena.

Final four Soulstones

  • First: the lower-left hidden-stair path.
  • Second: the path exposed behind the rotated room’s entrance.
  • Third: pillar above the curved balcony.
  • Fourth: the left false wall before the next door in the forward room after the basement.

Jarling location

The jar at the end of Imperial Palace Garden’s upper-right balcony.

Ozma and later phases

  • Clear embedded Chaos spears in phase one; prepare Fire, Water and Chaos cleansing and read strings at close range.
  • Distinguish physical and Chaos attacks in phase two, favouring evasion for Chaos; remain alert after a health bar empties.
  • Some ending routes add phase three. Reserve healing, counter Burst Attacks and use exhaustion openings for Brutal Attacks; three phases alone do not prove the true ending.

Ending choice and conclusion

With fifteen gold records and key bonus missions complete, confirm “I must know the truth” with Daphrona. If absent, recheck collection and missions before proceeding.

After victory, complete the memory-room interactions and scene. Ending prerequisites and dialogue choices distinguish the branches, not boss phase count alone.

Postgame cleanup and New Game+

Replay missions for missed collectibles before starting New Game+. Check the mission menus before committing to the second journey. All sixteen text routes are available; screenshots and full side-mission guides remain in progress.
