01Soulstone 1

  • Soulstone 1

Begin Bloodstained Sanctuary at the Great Temple, where Reese and Ozma were to be married.

A wedding-related vision plays on arrival.

Rest at the Nexus after the cinematic.

Enter the large gate toward the enemies inside.

First climb the courtyard's left stairs for a red material.

Descend and collect the white item right of the large gate.

Absorb the memory in the garden's center.

Collect the purple item on the right.

Take the blue item on the left stairs.

Drop from above for a purple item on the left.

Enter the large gate ahead for a Soulstone on the right beyond it.

02Soulstone 2

  • Soulstone 2

Take the small right door afterward. First climb the stairs on its left.

Defeat the elite mage above.

Continue toward the right staircase, but first absorb the memory on the left.

Descend the stairs for a chest.

Open the nearby gate to reconnect with the courtyard garden, or retrace your route.

Take the elevator in the previous room.

After descending, look up and shoot the enemy waiting to ambush you.

Collect the blue item beside the right cliff.

Follow the main route for a blue item before the right statue.

A Soulstone is visible high on the castle's left side. A Javelin shot is awkward from here; you can collect it later.

03About Reese II

  • About Reese II

Ambush the succubus by the castle entrance from range.

Enter the gate.

Climb the stairs and collect About Reese II beside the statue.

04Vilades's Confession

  • Vilades's Confession

If you missed the Soulstone below, look left outside and break the crates to reach its position.

Return to the route and rest at the new Nexus ahead.

Follow the main route, clearing enemies and collecting blue items along the way.

Absorb the memory inside the right doorway ahead.

Enter the neighboring door and defeat the duplicating elite.

Collect Vilades's Confession afterward.

05Jarling

  • Jarling

Rotate the two statues ahead to face one another.

The alignment opens the central passage.

Enter and clear the ambushers lining the corridor.

Watch for the laser-firing enemy ahead.

After the fight, enter the first door on the left.

Collect the blue item inside.

Search the small right room carefully for another ambush.

Its pot contains a Jarling.

06Soulstone 3

  • Soulstone 3

Climb the stairs and turn back for the chest behind the coffin.

Take the elevator ahead afterward.

Rest at the new Nexus above.

Collect the blue item beneath the right stairs.

Enter the large gate, the only route forward.

Explore right inside first.

Break the hidden wall on the right.

Defeat the enemies and collect the Soulstone inside.

07Temple Knight Scroll

  • Temple Knight Scroll

Climb all the way up the stairs. At the top, clear the right side and collect the purple item behind the enemies.

Collect the Temple Knight Scroll.

08About Reese III

  • About Reese III
  • Earth Purification Magic Scroll

Go left. The small left doorway contains no additional loot.

Continue forward and drop from the ledge.

Advance toward the purple item. Look up and attack the enemy poised to drop onto you.

Defeat it and collect the purple item.

Turn back and descend the stairs.

The lower staircase lets you open a shortcut gate to the previous Nexus.

Rest if needed, then return toward the elevator and stairs ahead. Descend the stairs first.

You reach the roofs. Drop from this position.

Collect the blue Earth Purification Magic Scroll below.

Go left for About Reese III on the beam.

09Soulstone 4

  • Soulstone 4

Descend the nearby ladder afterward.

Absorb the memory on the lower floor.

Do not drop farther after the memory. Climb back up.

At the pictured position, find the Soulstone behind the stairs ahead.

10Ghostly Beast Fang Necklace

  • Ghostly Beast Fang Necklace

Return to the roof and open the chest ahead.

Collect the Ghostly Beast Fang Necklace inside.

11Soulstone 5

  • Soulstone 5

Return to the elevator now.

Drop from this position, taking care not to miss the landing.

Climb left for a white item.

Continue up the final stone path.

Collect the blue item below on the left.

Return to the main route and approach the staircase.

A Soulstone sits high on the right. Shoot it now with Javelin or from above later.

12Resting before the boss

Climb the stairs carefully: enemies pressure you along the narrow route.

If you missed the Soulstone below, throw a Javelin from here. Do not walk toward it because the path is broken.

Absorb the memory on the right.

Drop from above afterward.

Continue forward and descend here.

Collect the purple item at the end.

Drop through the large hole onto the wooden beam.

Rest at the new Nexus below.

Open the chest in the nearby cell.

Leave into the large garden. Detonate the growths against its two large enemies.

Absorb the central memory.

Collect the red material nearby.

Collect the blue item on the side.

Take the purple item on the left steps.

Enter this doorway afterward.

Climb to the chest, collect it, then drop down.

Climb the left stairs.

Continue inside for a purple item as you reconnect with the hall.

Collect the golden material in the hall.

The rear gate reconnects with your arrival route and is optional.

Defeat the elite in the middle.

Take the left door afterward for a chest.

Open the other chest opposite.

Dodge this chest's explosion.

Climb the stairs after collecting it.

Rest at the Nexus above. The boss room is ahead.

13Boss encounter: Reese

  • Faded Memories Scroll

Enter to find Reese facing away in a white wedding dress.

She initially appears normal when she turns.

Then chaos-colored fluid seeps across her pale skin.

She becomes the bloodstained bride.

Reese has two forms. The first emphasizes magic; exhausting its health bar triggers the more melee-focused second form.

Her attacks are described below.

Attack ①:

Fast magic projectiles.

Attack ②:

Two diving attacks that can be deflected.

Attack ③:

A light wall blocks movement and can also stop her own attacks.

Attack ④:

Several magic-circle variations share similar tells. The lightning circle causes repeated follow-up explosions.

Attack ⑤:

A charged projectile becomes a magic circle on landing. Avoid its marked area.

Attack ⑥:

She rises and fires projectiles from above.

After the first health bar, her new form emphasizes melee but retains earlier spells.

Second form — Attack ①:

A five-hit melee combo.

Attack ②:

Two ordinary slashes followed by a thrown projectile.

Attack ③:

She enchants the scythe and dashes at you three times, ending with an explosion around herself.

Other moves largely reuse the first form's patterns.

Victory grants the Faded Memories Scroll.

Before fading, Reese asks Khazan to call out to Ozma.

A fallen feather glows and briefly restores her white bridal appearance. She asks Khazan to save Ozma, then disappears completely.

Khazan prepares to stop Ozma.
