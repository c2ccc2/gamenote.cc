Key progression points

Follow the opening path after the avalanche and activate the sword-shaped checkpoint. Once you receive the paired blades, use the first encounters to learn attacks, guarding, target lock and stamina use.

Keep some stamina in reserve against ordinary enemies. Defend through their attack sequence, answer with a short combo and watch for the transport order story item.

Continue through the snowfield. When the earlier checkpoint comes back into view, examine the cart above it to open a return shortcut.

Check your equipment and recovery items before the Snow Yeti King. The story moves into the next mission after the fight.

Route illustration

Things to watch for

  • Practice guarding and stamina management after getting your first weapon. Blocking also costs stamina, so leave enough to move away after a counterattack.
  • Check for the transport order story item after the early encounter. This is one checked pickup, not a complete list for the mission.
  • After opening the cart shortcut, you can return to the checkpoint to review your equipment before the boss.

Exploration illustration

Snow Yeti King: battle notes

  • Stay far enough away to read the wind-up. Dodge sideways when it charges, and counter after a longer attack string ends.
  • Later in the fight, watch for falling ice and a frontal ice attack. Evade first, then strike while reserving stamina for another guard or dodge.

Before the next mission

Follow the story after Yetuga. Trials of the Frozen Mountain now has route and collectible notes; continue through the mission directory.

Jarling location

After Jar Enthusiasts, return to Crash Site and break the small jar ahead of the Nexus.
