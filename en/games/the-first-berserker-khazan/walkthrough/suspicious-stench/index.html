Mine and poison-pool route

Activate Collapsed Mine and open its return shortcut. Check the distant Soulstone at the waterfall.

Continue past the large spider-egg chamber to Poisoned Depths. Speak to the Bard and prepare poison-cleansing items.

Follow rock islands and ramps to Bottom of the Cave, recover on dry ground and approach the boss.

Route illustration

Collectible landmarks

  • First Soulstone: use Javelin from the wooden platform edge on the crystal behind the waterfall.
  • Second: take the right crevice at the bottom of the Poisoned Depths ramp to the small rock island.
  • Third: turn back from Bottom of the Cave into the second poison pool and check behind rocks along the right wall.
  • Check the later ledge branch for the Blacksmith Union Permit and nearby Bard.

Exploration illustration

Jarling location

The small jar on the left of the giant spider-egg chamber.

Aratra: preparation and battle notes

  • Bring poison-cleansing items and resistant accessories. Explore barrel side paths before the fight to destroy small-spider entrances and reduce reinforcements.
  • Target forelegs or abdomen: breaking legs limits associated attacks; breaking the abdomen suppresses venom temporarily. Parts regenerate after its cave retreat.
  • Use Javelin on its back during a wall retreat. Delay counters for Burst strings and watch for grabs after webs.

Later mission illustration

After the battle and blacksmith services

After Aratra, complete Blacksmith’s Heirloom and recover the hammer for related services. Continue to the newly available sixth mission through the chapter navigation.
