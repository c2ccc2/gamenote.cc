Capital route

Check the alley behind Old Town’s elevator approach, then follow buildings and chaos-rock courtyards.

Pass the sewer waterfall, New Town and Church, open the shortcut and prepare for the central arena.

Soulstones, records and Bard

  • First: the Old Town left-alley end.
  • Second: the right alcove in the larger chaos-rock area.
  • Third: behind the lower sewer waterfall.
  • Fourth: behind the church statue.
  • Fifth: atop the short stairs beyond the church shortcut.
  • Gold records: the forward room in the later building and before the courtyard fountain; match Reese record names to your menu, as source numbering differs.
  • Bard: the right interior door in New Town’s split-door room.

Jarling location

The corner jar in the bedroom with the outer-wall hole; inspect it before leaving.

Hismar: battle notes

  • Break the horn, then target the forehead; use Javelin during weakened flight.
  • Purple glow warns of Chaos attacks; prepare cleansing and evade them.

Post-battle missions

Check Charon’s Chains, Disobedience and Birth of Evil. With three disk fragments, ask Daphrona for The Vow before the sanctuary.
