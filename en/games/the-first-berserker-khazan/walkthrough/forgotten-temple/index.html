Entering the temple

Activate the first Nexus. Check the initial right branch for a Vitality Tome, then return left and watch the archer above.

Enter the temple and inspect the branch right of the stairs. The entrance area includes the Bard merchant and a Netherworld Energy upgrade.

The boss gate is locked. Operate the nearby lever, descend by elevator and speak to Daphrona.

Route illustration

Stopping the rotating mechanisms

Align statues with others in their row. Use the raised platform and ladder to reach the first control device.

Continue deeper, clear enemies and solve the second statue puzzle. Check the exposed Soulstone and operate the second device.

Descend to the final device, stop the machinery, then return by elevator. Defeat the tough enemy, open the gate with the upper lever and rest before Viper.

Exploration illustration

Collectibles and characters

  • Soulstone one: behind breakable wooden barriers left of the pre-temple fork.
  • Soulstone two: the corner of the right branch before the entrance stairs, past two enemies.
  • Soulstone three: exposed by the second statue puzzle; break it with Javelin.
  • Daphrona offers Soulstone unleashing. Finish her dialogue and check the available upgrades.
  • After Jar Enthusiasts, return from Depths of the Forgotten Temple, pass the second rotating trap and check the small jar left of the descending stairs.

Viper: battle notes

  • Phase-one spear attacks have delayed starts. Guard when the strike arrives; a weapon throw can lead into a rush.
  • Watch for a glowing spear tip and a drawn-back stance before the grab. Move away rather than relying on normal guarding.
  • Phase two mixes fast dagger strikes with slower punches. A palm slammed into the ground warns of a following rock throw.
  • If needed, check the white summon point before the arena and spend the corresponding Circular Lacrima to summon a Spirit of Advocacy.

After the battle

Complete the story and hub dialogue after Viper, then continue to First Act of Revenge through the mission navigation. Return for the Jarling after completing its later prerequisite.

Later mission illustration

Jarling location

After the prerequisite, return to the temple depths and check left of the descending stairs beyond the second rotating trap.
