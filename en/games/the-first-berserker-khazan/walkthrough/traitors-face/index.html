01Meet Elamein and descend the broken bridges to the lower town

With the side missions cleared, begin the sixth main mission.

Speak to Elamein on arrival. The source did not establish whether her dialogue choices change anything.

Two soldiers guard loot in the house at the bottom-right end of the slope.

Follow the main path. The bridges collapse, forcing you down successive drops.

02Rooftop fork and blocking cart: find the Wanted Poster

At the lower fork, take the right route onto the roof for a blue item.

Continue left and collect the white roadside item.

Smash the cart blocking the path.

  • Wanted: Veiled Knives Bandits · Location list

Collect the wanted notice for the Veiled Knives bandits beside the road.

04Soldier at the left door and explosive chest: climb the indoor stairs

A tough soldier emerges from the left doorway. First eliminate the archer across from you to reduce the pressure.

Enter the left doorway after the fight and absorb the memory.

Collect the purple item behind the house.

Return and climb the stairs to a chest. Dodge its explosion after opening.

Take the other staircase and open the upper door.

Open the chest inside.

05Lower alley Nexus: aim at the relic from the house on the right

Descend toward the enemies below.

Collect the blue item in the left alley.

Return to the main road and rest at the Nexus ahead.

Take the right-hand route after resting.

Defeat the enemy bursting through this door and enter.

Climb inside and turn left outside the upper door. Use Javelin to knock down the red item.

  • Ghostly Relic · Location list

Collect the Ghostly Relic.

07Rooftop chest and drop: circle beneath the beams for the Soulstone

Climb farther for a rooftop chest.

Descend, then use the pictured corner to reach another rooftop.

Drop from this position.

Absorb the memory ahead.

Climb the ladder behind you.

After two climbs, leave the building. Both sides are accessible; drop right first for loot.

  • Soulstone 1 · Location list

A Soulstone sits on the beam inside.

09Below the left ladder: circle to the back door and climb to the top

Return after the Soulstone, climb the ladder and go left.

At the end, descend the ladder.

Continue straight for a blue item.

Explore the right branch for another blue item and a memory on its platform, then return.

Follow the small path behind the house.

Enter the small doorway.

  • Soulstone 2 · Location list

Climb the ladder to the Soulstone on the upper level.

11Chain bridge and castle drop: reach the inner Nexus

Break the bridge's chain to lower the crossing.

Drop from this point into the castle.

Continue dropping on the right and collect the purple item.

Rest at the Nexus to the left.

12Passage beside the locked gates: find the emperor's letter by the desk

Follow the pictured route; the iron gates on the right remain locked.

  • Grimy Imperial Letter · Location list

At the path's end, collect the Grimy Imperial Letter beside the table.

14Alley key and study: break the wall to find the jar

The narrow left path contains several enemies. Watch the archers.

Defeat the group guarding the right chest and obtain the key inside.

Use it on the gate behind you for another chest.

Return to the Nexus and open the previously locked iron gate.

Clear the study and break the wall at the corner. Leave the route down on the right for later.

  • Jarling · Location list
  • Bard Scroll · Location list

Inside the hidden area, collect the Bard Scroll and the Jarling in the corner.

17Descend through the wine cellar: break the stone wall beside the barrels

Leave and cross the wine cellar to absorb a memory ahead.

Turn back and take the route to the lower floor.

Open the iron gate below. The left route also connects and its room contains items.

Break the stone wall beside two barrels in the corridor.

  • Soulstone 3 · Location list

A Soulstone lies inside. Watch for an enemy ambush.

19Invisible sewer enemy: open the gate with the corner switch

Descend farther and open the next door.

The sewer contains invisible enemies. Defeat the first and collect the white item on the left.

At the fork ahead, the right branch holds a purple item.

Take the left branch and operate the corner mechanism to open the gate.

20Sewer shaft: speak to Andell and descend to the planks

Follow the path to its end and descend.

Speak to Andell on the left before dropping farther down.

Follow the descending ledges carefully to avoid falling into the void.

  • Soulstone 4 · Location list

A Soulstone is hidden behind the boards on this mandatory route.

22Elevator at the shaft's end: reach the upper Nexus

Take the elevator upward.

Rest at the Nexus above before the boss.

23Castle hall: Rangkus's hammer and firearm

The cinematic shows Tristan defending Pavel, Khazan's revenge target, against a powerful attacker.

Tristan is overpowered, and the attacker kills the defenseless Pavel.

The attacker is Rangkus. Seeing Khazan alive, he turns on him.

Rangkus does not change phases. Potent attacks can interrupt some moves, but respect his hammer combos.

Attack ①:

Four slow hits. Do not guard too early.

Attack ②:

Attack ③:

A charging firearm attack ending in a thrust.

Attack ④:

He charges the firearm and spins through a large area. Interrupt him if possible.

Attack ⑤:

An aerial slam with a visible leap.

Attack ⑥:

A sweep leads into aerial fire and a ground slam, followed by an almost arena-wide ground explosion. Time your dodge for the blast.

Attack ⑦:

A charged stomp resembling his other ground strikes.

Attack ⑧:

Three ranged shots without fire damage; these can be guarded.

24Speak to Tristan: uncover the truth about Pavel

Defeat Rangkus for equipment.

Tristan survives and is surprised to find Khazan alive.

He explains that Pavel pretended to betray Khazan to save him, clearing the misunderstanding too late.

The emperor feared the popularity Khazan gained by defeating Hismar and engineered the treason accusations.

You return to the hub, where Tristan joins you. The chapter ends.
