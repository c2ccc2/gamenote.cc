Hidden Paths

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

Buy the Glass Eye from Looter’s shop in the Save Tent for 450 at its listed price. Equip it to reveal hidden passages, then check each route for traversal abilities such as Climb or Soar.

Glass Eye acquisition and effect

During the main story

Hidden entrances
RegionEntrance landmark
Graven ValleyOnce you have Climb, check the upper-left corner of the spirit’s room for the hidden passage.
Autumn ForestAfter defeating the Forest King, enter the Golden Feather room. Take the hidden passage to Ilda’s right for a story page.
Burial VaultUse the Story Page 18/23 map in the linked walkthrough to locate the Dollmaker page. For the other passage, see Workshop Token 25/27. Bring Hover, Endless Climb and Wall Sling, then break the barrier inside.
Dollmaker's HouseUse the Workshop Token 26/27 map in the linked walkthrough to locate the room. Bring Slingshot and Endless Climb, then climb into the passage for the token.

Postgame Exploration

After the ending, unlock Soar in the Queen’s chambers and collect Mysterious Markings above the Ability Egg nest. Use these landmarks to find the five entrances marked on the map.

Soar entrances
RegionEntrance landmarkRoute after enteringInteractive map stop
Dollmaker's HouseSoar from the middle room on the top corridor.Follow the hidden passage to the end and collect the fragment.8
Night GardenSoar from the far-west room connecting to the Bog to reach the combat room above.Clear the enemies and take the upper passage to the fragment.9
Autumn ForestSoar from the room west of the time trial to enter the platforming route.Clear the platforming section and collect the fragment at the end.10
The DocksHead east from the tent near Whisper’s hut and Soar from the bridge.Leap from the top platform and move left and right to collect all 100 lights. If you miss one, follow the bottom-left prompt to retry.11
Desiccated CastleSoar from the nest to the right of the southeastern tent to enter the hidden tower.Climb to the top of the tower for the fragment.12
Night GardenWith all five fragments, return to the nest left of the lower tent.Watch the reunion at the nest.13
