The Deep · Maps

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-09-27

Scene Landmarks

Follow the passage to the left of the giant skull landmark. Use the minimap to identify room connections and inspect exits above and below.

Map Orientation and Landmarks

Regional Orientation
Landmark / NodeHow to Identify ItMap Reading Notes
Vessel passagesNarrow caves, steps and vertical waterways form the vessel route.Use the minimap connections; unlit space is not necessarily passable.
Spiked side passageA spiked branch shows a trinket-slot pickup message.This confirms one pickup, not the region's total.
Large skull backdropThe vessel passes horizontally along the left of a large skull.Use the skull as a landmark, not a new region name.
Land fight and campfireLater scenes include an on-foot fight and a campfire with Barge and the crew.Distinguish land and vessel sections; the gathering is not an achievement confirmation.
