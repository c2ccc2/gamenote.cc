The Docks

Guide in progress · Updated 2026-09-27

GAME NOTE CLASSIC · Well Dweller

★ The Docks — illustrated walkthrough

1. Enter the wooden platform area

The docks combine wooden platforms, stepped ledges and substantial vertical drops. Advance from platform to platform, checking side-wall spikes before jumping. Space remains beneath the upper route, so the first visible path is not necessarily every branch.

2. Find the captain and finish the first conversation

The indoor room with a round window and life rings contains the captain labelled Baji in the Chinese dialogue. He mentions missing crew members. Finish the conversation before checking the docks' branches. This is a quest lead, not proof of completion.

3. Move between hanging nets and spiked balls

Hanging nets alternate with spiked round obstacles in an aerial passage. Hazards sit both above and below. Move through the gaps toward safe platforms, and plan the return route before collecting something high above the spike-covered floor.

◆ The purple-tent save point

A purple tent on the wooden platform displays a save confirmation. Use it as a return node when exploring multiple branches. Check the route and supplies here before entering consecutive spike rooms.

4. Identify safe faces on suspended spike boards

Several suspended platforms cross a room whose floor and vertical board sides carry spikes. The recording passes above them. Distinguish safe landing faces from spikes; not every edge of a vertical board is a climbable wall.

★ The Docks — illustrated walkthrough

The map shows explored pale-purple rooms, the player at the right-hand end, several vertical branches and purple node markers. Recheck adjacent passages room by room. Black unexplored areas remain, so this is a progress snapshot rather than a complete map.

5. Return through the hanging-net passage

The return route crosses the hanging nets and spiked balls again. The commentary points out opening a locked connection from below. Document the first crossing separately from opening the return shortcut, then use the shorter route for remaining pickups.

6. The later fight along the waterside walkway

This encounter takes place on a horizontal wooden walkway above the water, with the enemy to the left and spikes farther along. Use the walkable space to adjust distance while watching the edge and follow-up moves. The frame does not establish the boss's formal name.

7. Dialogue after the encounter

After the fight, a fallen character speaks an apology and the arena transitions into a story scene. Record this as a post-battle node. Identify relationships through the dialogue rather than the character's appearance alone.

→ Return to the captain's room

The recording ends with a return to the earlier room containing the round window and life rings. This provides a revisit node for the missing-crew storyline. Without a completion message, the frame alone does not confirm a reward or vehicle unlock.

★ The Docks — preparations — illustrated walkthrough

→ Resupply at the docks before descending

The opening remains at the docks' purple tent, with a save confirmation and commentary advising another oil bottle. Keep this preparation stop in the docks walkthrough before the underwater section. The episode title does not place its first frame in the deep.
