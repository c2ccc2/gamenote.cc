The Bog

Guide in progress · Updated 2026-09-27

GAME NOTE CLASSIC · Well Dweller

★ The Bog — illustrated walkthrough

1. Drop through the entrance shaft

Check the vertical passage near the bog entrance. The recording drops from the upper route into a lower branch, with a bubble visible in the shaft. Consult the map before continuing horizontally so you do not miss the lower passage.

2. Check below the right-hand platform

Drop from the right edge of the grassy platform and inspect the lower ledge near the pot. An enemy remains above, so check your landing first. The glowing pickup is visible, but its item name has not been confirmed.

3. Speak with Ilda

Ilda stands on a platform with large wagon wheels in the background. Her dialogue mentions the palace above. Finish the conversation before exploring the surrounding passages; this dialogue does not establish that the palace is currently accessible.

◆ A trinket-slot pickup in the shaft

The recording shows a trinket-slot acquisition message while the character rebounds upward in the narrow shaft. Use the accompanying minimap to locate the explored room. This confirms one pickup, not the total number of slots in the bog.

4. The timed room with floor spikes

The next section enters a separate brick-walled challenge room with a timer and floor spikes. Check the platform spacing and keep landings clear of the spikes. This frame shows the challenge in progress, not completion or a reward.

◆ A glowing pickup on a small platform

Back in the bog, a glowing pickup sits on the small platform inside a narrow passage. Watch the low ceiling and establish a safe landing before moving onward. Its exact item name is not readable in this frame.

5. Ilda beside the campfire

Ilda appears again, seated by the campfire. Record this separately from the earlier roadside conversation; these are two encounters with the same NPC. Both dialogue locations can support later quest notes.

★ The Bog — illustrated walkthrough

The map shows pale-yellow bog rooms connecting to a blue area on the right. Compare the player marker, horizontal passages and upward branches. Black unexplored space remains, so this is a progress snapshot rather than a complete map.

6. Watch the large skull during the fight

During the later encounter, the large skull in the background moves into the arena; the commentary explicitly points out this movement. Watch its direction and preserve room to dodge sideways. The boss name has not been confirmed from this frame.

※ Watch both the ground and the swarm above

A later combat frame contains the grounded skull, a swarm overhead and round projectiles approaching from the right. Check above and ahead before moving. Damage values, exact phase thresholds and an optimal strategy have not been established.

7. Revisit walls after the climbing tutorial

The in-game tutorial explains that holding jump against a wall allows climbing. Stamina is limited and recovers on solid ground. Plan stops on stable platforms rather than assuming you can climb indefinitely.

→ Revisit the high route beside the purple tent

The later recording returns to the purple tent, displays the bog region title and confirms a save. Recheck previously inaccessible high branches using the climbing instruction. This does not establish that every remaining pickup has been collected.
