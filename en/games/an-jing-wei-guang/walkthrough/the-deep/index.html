The Deep

Guide in progress · Updated 2026-09-27

GAME NOTE CLASSIC · Well Dweller

★ The Deep — illustrated walkthrough

1. Enter the narrow underwater passages

This section explores underwater in a spherical vehicle with a headlight. Fish-like enemies and changes in elevation occupy the passages. Slow down to examine exits and enemy movement before crossing a narrow opening. The existing The Deep directory is retained while the Deep/Depths naming difference remains unresolved.

2. Vehicle prompts and headlight direction

The frame displays an RT prompt beside the vehicle and its forward light beam. Check the wall ahead and the minimap while adjusting direction. This records the controller prompt actually shown, not a universal binding for every platform.

◆ A trinket-slot pickup in a spiked branch

The underwater side passage has diagonal wall spikes and a spiked floor. An acquisition message confirms a trinket slot. Check the narrow entrance and allow space to turn when leaving. One confirmed pickup does not establish the area's total.

3. Check the upward passage beside the terrain

The route turns from a low horizontal channel toward a stepped passage above and to the right. Check the minimap for an upward connection before redirecting the vehicle. Unlit space is not automatically a usable exit.

◆ A collectible beside the vertical channel

A glowing pickup appears beside a small ledge near the lower end of an ascending water channel. Inspect side recesses instead of travelling straight up the main shaft. Without an item panel, its exact name and quantity remain unconfirmed.

※ Mark the branch you cannot reach yet

At a room with a spiked ball and vertical flow, the commentary says the upper pickup cannot currently be reached. Mark the location and continue along accessible routes. Do not count it as collected or invent a required ability from this frame.

4. Inspect the passage near the giant skull

The later underwater route passes left of a giant skull. Use the map's room connections to identify the exit and inspect the passage edges. The skull is a visual landmark, not proof that a new named region has begun.

5. Back on foot: spear-shaped projectiles

After the underwater section, the recording enters a room for combat on foot. The enemy appears from the upper-left and spear-shaped projectiles cross the arena. Watch incoming attacks from above and leave room to dodge rather than continuing to think in vehicle controls.

The enemy later lands while red projectiles remain overhead and to the right. The commentary recommends approaching after it pauses. Check projectile landing positions before closing in. A complete attack cycle, damage values and formal name have not been established.

6. The captain and crew by the fire

After combat, the story moves to a campfire scene with the captain and several hat-wearing characters. Finish the dialogue before documenting the quest result. The group appearing together is not, by itself, an achievement-unlock confirmation.
