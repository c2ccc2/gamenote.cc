The Well

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-09-27

Area at a Glance

Goal
Leave through the right-hand skull exit; inspect the descending water channel on a later visit.
Ability / Limits
This page covers the starting route, not a new ability unlock.
Return Visits
Return to the starting area later to inspect the descending route.

Map

Area overview

Start inside the large skull. Leave through the right exit and continue toward Night Garden.

1. Leave the skull and enter the garden

Leave the large skull through the exit on the right and continue into Night Garden.

After leaving the starting area, enter Night Garden and explore its early rooms.

2. Return to the start and continue downward

Later, return to the starting area and follow the water channel downward.
