Autumn Forest

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

Collection Notes

  • Ferris wheel spirit: Go right two rooms from the left Autumn Forest tent, use the Ferris wheel to go up two rooms, then head left to the time trial statue. Finish the trial, enter the reward room and break the spirit statue.
  • Mushroom puzzle spirit: From the right tent, go down and left. Solve the mushroom room and enter the next one. Use Slingshot to bounce the matchstick off the mushrooms and use it as a foothold. Bring Climb and Hover, then break the statue beyond the puzzle.
  • Return both freed spirits to the nest left of the lower Night Garden tent.
  • Tent target game: Bring Slingshot. Hit the gold target for a trinket slot (illustrated slot entry 42) and the narrow light-blue target for Blue Bow. Keep an oil vial in reserve while wearing the bow so it can spend the vial to heal you when you take a lethal hit.
  • Doll’s Head: Bring Climb to the orb puzzle branch. Reach the top ahead of the orb, then strike it upward to light the pillar and collect the trinket. It increases attack reach when equipped.

Reference Route

  • The Autumn Forest gate has two switches. On the Night Garden side, go west from the tent before the Lookout Tower, clear the combat room and activate the switch. You can pick up Glass Shard nearby.
  • On the Hunter’s Cabin side, return to the room where you took food in the prologue. Use Slingshot and Hover to reach the upper exit. Go east in the next room to the tent, then head left to the two moving cages.
  • Stand on the first cage and fire the matchstick through the narrow gap into the second. Use it to climb up and exit left. In the next room, take the upper exit at the far left and continue left to the second switch. With the gate open, climb through the top exit into Autumn Forest.
  • Bounce up on the mushrooms, cross the spike pit to the right and follow the festival decorations. Ride the Ferris wheel up and take the top exit. After the third orb puzzle, pass the festive hat vendor and continue west to the festival tent.
  • If the route west of the festival tent is blocked, take the passage below it. Turn left at the right-hand barrier, solve the fourth orb puzzle and continue left to slam through the wooden floor. Clear the enemies below and take the right exit to loop around to Ilda’s acorn campfire.
  • Go left from the campfire to open the shortcut, then climb to the top and open the barrier beside the festival tent from the far side. Save and head west to the Forest King. After the fight, go into the room on the right, speak to Ilda and collect the Golden Feather.
  • A story page is hidden right of Ilda in the feather room; check the climbing route.

Regional Orientation

The orange region sits near the top of the map; the Forest King arena is a main landmark.

Puzzle Solutions

Hit every pillar in one attempt; floor, wall or off-screen contact resets the orb.Two-pillar room: send the orb sideways so it rebounds between both pillars. Reference video: 00:00.Three-pillar room: hit the orb right toward the floor pillar, then jump over it onto the first ledge on the right. Hit the falling orb left toward the wall pillar and stay on the ledge. When it returns beneath the ceiling pillar, strike upward. Reference video: 00:16.Third room: stand to the right of the orb and hit it left into the slanted mushroom. As the orb drops, bounce off the middle mushroom and strike right in midair, aiming at the upper-left of the right bumper. Reference video: 00:34.Fourth room: hit left into the slanted mushroom. While the orb rebounds toward the ceiling mushroom above its start, climb onto the upper platform. Strike left again toward the higher slanted mushroom. Move to the left of the pillar, past the left ceiling mushroom, and hit the returning orb into the pillar. Reference video: 00:49.Doll’s Head branch: hit the orb left, then climb to the upper part of the room before it arrives. Strike upward when it passes directly beneath the pillar, then go through the opened barrier to collect the trinket. Reference video: 01:12.

Bird spirit locations

  1. Bird spirit 1：From the left tent, go two rooms right, two up by the ferris wheel, then left to the time trial.
  2. Bird spirit 2：From the right tent, go down and left through the mushroom puzzle; use a Slingshot platform and mushrooms in the next room.
