Graven Valley

Guide in progress · Updated 2026-09-27

GAME NOTE CLASSIC · Well Dweller

★ Graven Valley — illustrated walkthrough

1. The bell mechanism and outlined platforms

A hanging bell appears above a graveyard route. Outlined platform positions lie to the right, while spikes cover the floor and part of the wall. Do not assume an outlined surface is currently solid; inspect the mechanism and platform changes first.

★ Graven Valley — illustrated walkthrough

The map shows several branches in the pale-coloured area and a connection to the red area on the left. The player marker sits at the right end near slanted platforms. Keep these neighbouring areas separate when documenting the route.

2. The announcer on the wooden stage

The crown-decorated wooden stage is a story stop. The first dialogue box identifies the speaker as the announcer, and a cage hangs above the stage. Finish the conversation before checking either side. This is not a boss-defeat scene.

3. Speak with Egbert in the cage

On a later visit to the wooden frame, the dialogue identifies Egbert. The characters in the two hanging cages look different and should be tracked separately. This confirms a conversation and location, not a completed rescue.

※ Ground targets and flying enemies together

A low passage contains a ground target and two flying enemies overhead. The commentary recommends removing the airborne threats first. Keep room to move before approaching the ground target, and do not mistake visual descriptions for official enemy names.

4. Recheck elevation changes between the graves

The route passes several gravestones and stepped platforms, with another wooden structure at the right. Check the sides and lower branches of the known horizontal route. Watch the ceiling before attempting to climb out of a low passage.

5. Returning to the stage before combat

The later recording revisits the crown-decorated stage, now with a large transformed creature in front. Distinguish this visit from the initial announcement and the cage conversations so the walkthrough does not merge separate events.

★ Graven Valley — illustrated walkthrough

The character stands on the central board while spikes cover both sides of the arena. The large enemy has multiple arms and releases red round attacks. Track the usable platform width and approaching attacks before dealing damage. The commentator's personal round count is not a fixed phase rule.

★ Graven Valley revisited — illustrated walkthrough

◆ Before the docks: revisit the graveyard bell

The opening still takes place in Graven Valley, revisiting a bell with an outlined platform connecting the upper terrain. Keep this section in the valley walkthrough rather than assigning it to the docks solely because of the video title.

→ Continue from the other side of the locked passage

The recording reaches the opposite side of a previously locked passage, a connection explicitly described by the commentary. Slanted boards and small platforms bridge the terrain, with another branch below. Check the minimap before heading upward toward the docks.
