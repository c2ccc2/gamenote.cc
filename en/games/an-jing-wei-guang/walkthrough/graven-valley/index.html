Graven Valley

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-09-27

Area at a Glance

Goal
Observe the bell mechanism and platform changes, then explore the stage and cage locations.
Ability / Limits
An outlined platform is not necessarily solid; inspect the mechanism before crossing.
Return Visits
Revisit the bell and the opposite side of the locked passage; inspect lower and side branches.

Map

★ Graven Valley — illustrated walkthrough

1. The bell mechanism and outlined platforms

Outlined platforms sit to the right of the hanging bell, with spikes on the floor and wall. Inspect the mechanism and platform changes before crossing; an outline is not necessarily a solid surface.

★ Graven Valley — illustrated walkthrough

Check the local map's pale-coloured branches and connection to the red area on the left. Locate the player marker at the right-hand end using the nearby slanted platforms.

2. The announcer on the wooden stage

Speak with the announcer at the crown-decorated wooden stage. A cage hangs above; finish the conversation before checking either side of the stage.

3. Speak with Egbert in the cage

Return to the cage frame and speak with Egbert. The characters in the two hanging cages differ; inspect their conversations and nearby passages separately.

※ Ground targets and flying enemies together

The low passage contains a ground target and two flying enemies. Clear the airborne threats first, then approach the ground target while preserving room to move.

4. Recheck elevation changes between the graves

Move between the graves and stepped platforms, checking ledge sides and lower branches. Watch the ceiling before jumping out of a low passage.

5. Returning to the stage before combat

A large transformed creature appears in front of the crown-decorated stage on a later visit. Follow this combat segment after the initial announcement and cage conversations.

★ Graven Valley — illustrated walkthrough

Use the central stage board while spikes cover the ground on both sides. Watch the large enemy's red round attacks and look for an opening before dealing damage.

★ Graven Valley revisited — illustrated walkthrough

◆ Before the docks: revisit the graveyard bell

Revisit the Graven Valley bell and inspect how the high transparent platform links the terrain. Complete this return visit before heading toward the docks.

→ Continue from the other side of the locked passage

Continue from the opposite side of the previously locked passage. Check the slanted boards, small platforms and lower branch on the minimap before climbing toward the docks.
