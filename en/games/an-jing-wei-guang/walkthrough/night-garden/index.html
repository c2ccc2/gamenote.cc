Night Garden

Guide in progress · Updated 2026-09-27

GAME NOTE CLASSIC · Well Dweller

Boss

The Groundskeeper

★ Recorded route

In Night Garden, learn to save and use oil bottles before continuing to the Groundskeeper. Oil bottles relight the match, temporarily strengthen attacks and refill at save points.
Oil bottles relight the match, briefly strengthen attacks and refill at save points.
Continue forward to encounter the Groundskeeper and begin the boss fight.

1. Early garden exploration and the save point

Find the tent in the early garden section to save your game and open the tent menu.

2. Oil-bottle instructions and bubble platforms

Oil bottles relight the match and briefly strengthen attacks. Refill them at save points before continuing your exploration.

Continue through the bubbles and higher platforms, checking routes on both levels.

Pick up the trinket slot along the route. Do not confuse it with the heart-shaped container, the higher glowing object or the Hunter's Pendant.

3. Encounter the Groundskeeper

The Groundskeeper encounter follows the oil-bottle tutorial. Check your supplies before the fight.

Pink petal-shaped projectiles appear around the Groundskeeper. Watch their coverage during the fight.

After defeating the Groundskeeper, continue toward Hunter's Cabin for the next exploration segment.

4. Later exploration: the cabin and Looter

Speak with Looter in the cabin segment and continue to the flaming-vessel tutorial.

Throw a flaming vessel, then strike it in midair to detonate it. Practise the action before continuing through the cabin.

The B prompt reflects the creator's controller binding at that moment, not a keyboard or custom-binding instruction.

5. The hanging cages and Hunter's Pendant

The cabin route combines hanging cages, bubbles and spikes. Watch for safe footholds and changes in elevation.

Obtain the Hunter's Pendant. It extends the healing timing window rather than increasing maximum health.

6. The local map and return to the garden

Open the map to review explored rooms and branches, then check routes you may have missed.

After the cabin tutorial, return to the garden and inspect the route near the purple obstacle.
