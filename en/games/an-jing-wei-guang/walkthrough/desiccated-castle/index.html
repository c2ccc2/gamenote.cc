★ Desiccated Castle — illustrated walkthrough

1. Use the purple tent as the starting point

The recording starts near a purple tent inside the castle and confirms a save. Use this node to plan return routes before exploring branches. The existing Desiccated Castle directory is retained; its correspondence with the Chinese video title still needs a clear region-title frame.

◆ A collectible branch blocked by stone

In a low-ceilinged room, the commentary points out a collectible above that is blocked by stone. Check the ground switch or other mechanism and then observe changes to the upper route. No inventory panel appears, so the item name and quantity are left unconfirmed.

2. The timed challenge in a spiked shaft

The challenge climbs a narrow shaft lined with spikes on both walls. At this point the commentary advises climbing toward the left. Follow the available safe wall sections without letting the timer distract you from the spikes. This is not a completion frame.

3. The suspended wooden boat and projectiles

A wooden boat-shaped object hangs from a chain inside the castle, with blue bottle-shaped projectiles nearby. Watch its movement and projectile direction while keeping horizontal space. Appearance alone does not establish a boss identity or a completed NPC quest.

4. Looter mentions the docks

The following dialogue identifies Looter and says he plans to visit the docks to find his sister after finishing here. This provides a story lead toward the docks. Finish the current conversation and remaining rooms first; a future plan does not mean he is already at the docks.

◆ A high story page beside the chain mechanism

A glowing story page is visible in the upper-left of a chain-mechanism room. Spikes cover the left wall and a projecting platform. Judge the jump from a safe ledge and approach without touching the spikes. The page's story chapter is not established without its menu.

5. Revisit the lower pickup branch

The later route returns to a grassy platform beneath a low ceiling, where a green pickup icon is visible. Compare the minimap with your return path and inspect the lower edges of platforms. The frame lacks a name panel, so only the pickup node is recorded.

※ Different safe surfaces in the moonlit room

A separate upper platform in the moonlit room is covered with spikes, while the lower floor provides a continuous route. Confirm the lower exit first before exploring the higher branch. Not every visible platform is a safe resting point.

6. The gravestone dialogue above the water

The dialogue beside the waterside gravestone identifies the protagonist and mentions a long-forgotten curse. This is a story node, not proof that a hidden ending has been unlocked. Ending conditions require their own subsequent trigger checks.

→ The nest platform and the next tutorial

The recording then reaches a nest-shaped platform with a glowing tutorial object and proceeds into a separate practice space. Complete the interaction and follow the in-game instructions. The ability's formal name was not clearly read, so existing ability records are not renamed.
