Midnight Drench

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

Collection Notes

  • Bubble route trinket slot: Return with Slingshot and use illustrated slot entry 22 to locate the route. Fire the matchstick at the farthest bubble first, then bounce across the remaining bubbles in order to reach the slot.
  • Spirit from the castle approach: From the central Desiccated Castle tent, go left three rooms, down two and left two. The statue on this route is in Midnight Drench.
  • Time trial spirit: Go right two rooms from the upper Midnight Drench tent toward Webdrench Inn. Finish the trial, enter the reward room and break the spirit statue.
  • Spider’s Web: Enter Midnight Drench from the passage beyond the Lady of the Drains, then go down one room and right two rooms to the bubble puzzle. Fire the matchstick right through the bubbles, then bounce only on the final bubble beneath the trinket. Equipping it lets you launch Flaming Vessels with Slingshot.
  • Slanted rooftop page: Go slightly right and up from the upper Midnight Drench tent, then right two rooms to the slanted rooftops. Cross them and check the hidden wall at the far right. Wall-jump up inside the passage to collect the page.
  • Eastern page: Go right from the central Desiccated Castle tent to the moving pillar. Use Slingshot to reach its top while it is lowered, then take the right-hand shaft up. Leave the next room to the right for eastern Midnight Drench. Take the first passage up and cross using bubbles and Hover. Both pages belong to The Witch’s Bargain; compare room maps in illustrated entries 9 and 10.

Reference Route

  • Shrine route: After learning Hover, return to Midnight Drench from southern Graven Valley or northeastern Desiccated Castle. Find the room with a floating bubble in the middle, bounce across and take the right exit.
  • Ride the bubbles to the top of the next room and take the upper-right exit to the save tent. Continue east and climb the bubbles to strike the switch at the top. From there, go up and left to the Shrine of the Seven Sons arena.
  • After defeating the Shrine, go left to pick up the Golden Feather and use the nearby switch to open a shortcut to Desiccated Castle.
  • Escaping the Innkeeper returns you to Midnight Drench.
  • After the Graven Valley rescue, drop through the floor beyond Egbert to reach the far side of Midnight Drench. Enter the room directly below, then descend again through its lower-left exit.
  • Head left into Desiccated Castle and drop to the lower room. Continue left to the east gate switch, activate it, then save at the entrance tent.

Regional Orientation

The dark-blue region; use the Shrine arena to orient the feather route.

Bird spirit locations

  1. Bird spirit 1：From the castle center tent, go three rooms left, two down and two left.
  2. Bird spirit 2：From the upper tent, go two rooms right toward Webdrench Inn. Bring Climb and Hover and break the spirit statue in the reward room after clearing the trial.
