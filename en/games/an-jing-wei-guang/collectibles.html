Collectible categories

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

Postgame collection checklist

  • Trinkets · Check all 32, including shop purchases and spirit rewards.
  • Spirits · Rescue all 20 and return them to the Night Garden nest.
  • Story pages · Check the three books for 9, 8 and 6 pages.
  • Workshop Tokens · Check all 27 locations by area. Apply the upgrades at a tent for Fully Stocked.
  • Trinket slots · Check trial reward rooms, shops and pickups gated by late-game abilities.
  • Time trials · Check whether you claimed the rewards from all ten trials.
  • Reunion ending · After learning Soar, collect all five Spirit Fragments and return to the nest.

Find the last missing items

  1. At a save tent, open Map Markers and buy the category you are missing. Its markers cover items in rooms you have already entered.
  2. If the map shows no marker, check unexplored branches and reward rooms you may have skipped after a trial.
  3. Equip Rusted Compass when checking a specific room. It signals when the current room contains a collectible. If you get a signal, check for a hidden wall or an upper passage.

Rusted Compass pickup route · Map marker reference

Postgame collection order

  1. Pick up the Queen’s Coin in her chambers, then return to a tent to buy missing trinkets and slots at a discount.

    The Queen’s Coin

  2. Rescue the remaining spirits by area and return all 20 to the Night Garden nest. The final spirit reward counts toward the 32 trinkets.

    Spirit location checklist

  3. Collect all 32 trinkets to open the western chamber door, learn Soar and pick up Mysterious Markings. Revisit high slots you can now reach.

    Trinket location checklist

  4. Follow the markings to find all five Spirit Fragments after learning Soar, then return to the nest for the reunion ending.

    Spirit Fragments

  5. For remaining collectibles, check the 23 pages across all three books, then use the workshop menu to identify trinkets that still need upgrades.
