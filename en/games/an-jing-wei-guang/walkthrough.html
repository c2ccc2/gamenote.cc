Walkthrough index

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

Where to go when stuck

The Drains elevator is still broken

Find all three elevator parts and return to the lift beside the central tent. If you are one part short, use the repair guide to identify the branch you missed.

Elevator repair guide

The Docks gate is still closed

Collect all three gate stones and follow the map marker back to the gate. After the fight beyond it, continue to the campfire and finish the crew member’s conversation with Barge to gain vessel access.

The Docks walkthrough

You run out of stamina halfway up a long wall

Rescue Thimble and Thistle in the Deep, then return to Barge at the Docks shipyard for Endless Climb before trying the wall again.

How to get Endless Climb

You cannot enter Queen’s Castle

Before returning to the Bog, check all seven Golden Feathers. Check the steps after each fight: go west after the Shrine of the Seven Sons and attend Squinton’s party after the Gravedigger.

All seven Golden Feather locations

Where to revisit after learning an ability

Slingshot → Autumn Forest tent

Play the target minigame with Slingshot. The gold target gives a trinket slot, and the narrow light-blue target gives Blue Bow.

Area walkthrough

Hover → Lookout Tower gap

Bring Climb and Slingshot too. Fire the matchstick through the narrow gap and bounce off it to reach the slot above. See illustrated trinket slot entry 35.

Area walkthrough

Torpedo → The Deep trinket slots

Use illustrated trinket slot entry 49 to find the Torpedo route. Entry 50 is also in the Deep and needs no extra ability, so check both while you are there.

Area walkthrough

Endless Climb → Burial Vault

Bring Hover back to Burial Vault for illustrated trinket slot entry 80. A Workshop Token in a hidden passage also needs both abilities.
