Spirits

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

All 20 spirits by area

After breaking the statues, return the spirits to the nest west of the lower Night Garden tent.

Nest reward milestones

  • Return 2 spirits · Tiny Witch Sculpture
  • Return 12 spirits · Pearl Jewelry
  • Return 20 spirits · Paper Clip with String

Missing the spirit reward?

  1. Check that all 20 spirits have been returned to the nest. If the return count is short, go back to the nest and wait to collect the reward before leaving.
  2. If you returned all 20 on an older build and Paper Clip with String is still missing, update the game and load the same save. The October 2 Steam patch says loading the updated build should restore missing spirit rewards to your inventory.

October 2 patch notes

Four spirits in time trial reward rooms

After completing each trial, enter the reward room and break the spirit statue.

  • Hunter's Cabin
  • The Bog
  • Midnight Drench
  • Autumn Forest

Night Garden（3）

  1. From the start tent, go up, left and down the shaft. Follow the bubbles to the challenge room and clear it.
  2. Use Slingshot to reach the bird statue just left and above the bottom tent.
  3. From the top tent, travel left through the edge of Lookout Tower, descend and open the gate above the Well entrance. Hover is required.

Hunter's Cabin（1）

  1. From the central tent, go three rooms right and three up to the trial statue. Bring Slingshot, Climb and Hover, then break the spirit statue in the reward room.

The Bog（2）

  1. From the left tent, go one room right, one down and one left. Clear the trial and break the spirit statue in the reward room.
  2. From the top tent, travel two rooms left and two rooms up, then clear the enemy challenge. Hover is required.

The Drains（1）

  1. From the center tent, go right and ride the updraft to the platform on the left. Wind Ride is required.

Graven Valley（1）

  1. From the valley tent, go three rooms right and then down. Cross the illusory wall toward the upper-left side.

Midnight Drench（2）

  1. From the castle center tent, go three rooms left, two down and two left.
  2. From the upper tent, go two rooms right toward Webdrench Inn. Bring Climb and Hover and break the spirit statue in the reward room after clearing the trial.

Lookout Tower（2）

  1. From the bottom tent, go down and right into the bubble challenge room.
  2. From the bottom tent, go left and up three rooms to the spiked fork, then continue right. Three flight-time increases are required.

Autumn Forest（2）

  1. From the left tent, go two rooms right, two up by the Ferris wheel, then left to the trial. Bring Climb and Hover, then break the spirit statue in the reward room.
  2. From the right tent, go down and left through the mushroom puzzle; use a Slingshot platform and mushrooms in the next room.

The Docks（2）

  1. From the lower-left tent, go right, up-right and right. Drop through the hidden wall near the center.
  2. From the right tent, go up three rooms and check the platform at the left end.

Dollmaker's House（1）

  1. Directly above the top tent in Dollmaker's House.

Burial Vault（2）

  1. From the right tent, go left twice and take the upper-right exit. Use a Slingshot platform on the moving wall.
  2. From the right tent, go left, down, left, up and right. Place a Slingshot platform among the spikes and launch upward.

Desiccated Castle（1）

  1. From the top tent, go right and up. Find the illusory wall below the statue and use a Slingshot platform to rise.
