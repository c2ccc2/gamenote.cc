Collection Notes

  • 23 pages form three books: The Witch's Bargain (9), The Dollmaker (8) and The Lookout Tower (6).
  • Check the Pages menu by book; collection remains possible after the Queen fight.
  • The Witch’s Bargain (9 pages), in book order: Night Garden (walkthrough 1), Desiccated Castle (5), Graven Valley (6), Desiccated Castle (8), Midnight Drench (9–10), Hunter’s Cabin (20), Desiccated Castle (21), Queen’s Castle (23). Page 4 is available after the Queen evasion sequence.
  • The Dollmaker (8 pages): Hunter’s Cabin (walkthrough 2), the Bog (3), Autumn Forest (13), the Bog (15), Dollmaker’s House (16–17), Burial Vault (18), Dollmaker’s House (19). After the Forest King, check the hidden passage to Ilda’s right in the Golden Feather room.
  • The Lookout Tower (6 pages): Night Garden (walkthrough 4), Graven Valley (7), Lookout Tower (11–12), the Deep (14), the Bog (22). The Deep page is available once you unlock the submarine and enter the region.
  • The Witch’s Bargain, page 5 (entry 9): From the upper Midnight Drench tent, go slightly right and up, then go right two rooms to the slanted rooftops. Cross them, enter the hidden wall at the far right and climb to the page.
  • The Witch’s Bargain, page 6 (entry 10): Head right from the central Desiccated Castle tent to the moving pillar. Use Slingshot to reach the top while the pillar is lowered, then take the right-hand shaft up. Exit right into eastern Midnight Drench, take the first passage up, and cross the gap with bubbles and Hover.
  • The Dollmaker, pages 5, 6 and 8 (entries 16, 17 and 19): All three are in the Dollmaker’s House. Use Hover and Climb for page 5. Bring Slingshot, Endless Climb and Hover for page 6, and fire the matchstick from the right platform before hovering across. Use the room map to find page 8.
  • The Witch’s Bargain, page 9 (entry 23): From the right-hand Queen’s Castle tent, go left two rooms, down one and left one. Bring Endless Climb and Hover.
  • The Witch’s Bargain, page 4 (entry 8): Finish evading the Queen in Desiccated Castle, then return with Slingshot and Climb. Use the room screenshot in entry 8 to locate the page.
  • The Witch’s Bargain, page 8 (entry 21): In Desiccated Castle, trigger the floor mechanism with Slingshot and ride the right-hand pillar up. When it lines up with the spikes jutting out from the left, use the matchstick as a foothold to jump across.
  • The Lookout Tower, page 5 (entry 14): Board the submersible left of the Docks tent and follow the second gate route to the bottom, then surface on the far side. Look for the page on the way to the second lever.
  • The Dollmaker, page 3 (entry 13): After defeating the Forest King, enter the Golden Feather room and check the hidden passage to Ilda’s right.
  • The Dollmaker, page 1 (entry 2): In Hunter’s Cabin, get onto the top of the moving spike block and ride it up to the hidden passage with the page.
  • The Witch’s Bargain, page 3 (entry 6): Bring Climb and Slingshot to Graven Valley. Use the illustrated firing position to shoot the matchstick at the bell, then collect the page.
  • The Dollmaker, page 4 (entry 15): Bring Slingshot and Climb to the Bog. In the room left of the tent, stand on the highest step at the left and fire the matchstick toward the wall with the page on the right.
  • The Dollmaker, page 7 (entry 18): Use the Burial Vault room map to locate the hidden passage. Once inside, picking up the page takes no extra ability.
  • The Lookout Tower, page 6 (entry 22): Return to the Bog with Endless Climb. Go right from the first tent and check the long tunnel leading to the Drains.
  • Numbers in parentheses match the illustrated walkthrough’s global order. Use them to find the room screenshots.
