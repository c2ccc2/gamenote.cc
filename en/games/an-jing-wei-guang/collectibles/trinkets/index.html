Collection Notes

  • There are 32 trinkets from exploration and Looter’s shop; check effects and slot costs before equipping.
  • 1 slot: Spider’s Web.
  • 3 slots: Tiny Witch Sculpture, Bird-Shaped Button.
  • 4 slots: Skull Keychain, Hunter’s Locket, Molar Tooth, Fish Hook, Glass Shard, Pearl Jewelry, Bundle of Hair, Bottle Cap, Glass Eye, Melted Candle, Ember Vial, Doll’s Head, Insect Wing, Hair Pin, Blue Bow, Broken Bell, Burnt Shoelace.
  • 5 slots: Rusty Fork, Nails with Twine, Scrap of Fabric, Paper Clip with String, Weathered Coin, Postage Stamp, Cracked Marble, Rusted Thimble, Rusted Compass, Golden Needle.
  • 6 slots: Silver Spoon, Silver Ring.
  • Hunter’s Locket: Pick it up in Hunter’s Cabin. It needs no extra ability and gives you a larger window to heal.
  • Glass Eye: Buy it from Looter in the Save Tent for 450 at its listed price. Equip it to reveal hidden passages.
  • Hair Pin: In the Bog, bounce on the bubbles in the order they appear to open the barrier. After eight seconds without attacking, your next hit deals double damage.
  • Burial Vault and Night Garden: Golden Needle surrounds you with spikes below 25% HP. Bundle of Hair sends out embers when you slam the ground. Neither requires an extra ability. See entries 4 and 6 in the reference for room maps.
  • The Drains: Melted Candle requires Wind Ride and grants two seconds of invulnerability after healing. See reference entry 8.
  • Graven Valley: Broken Bell requires no extra ability and refunds a Flaming Vessel that misses. Cracked Marble requires Slingshot and Climb, and slightly increases damage at full health. See reference entries 9 and 10.
  • Desiccated Castle: Silver Spoon requires Slingshot and Climb and briefly shields you while you attempt to heal. Burnt Shoelace requires Climb and leaves embers at top running speed. See entries 13 and 14.
  • Midnight Drench and Graven Valley: Spider’s Web requires Slingshot and Climb in Midnight Drench and lets you launch Flaming Vessels with Slingshot. Molar Tooth requires Climb in Graven Valley and emits embers while sliding. See entries 18 and 21.
  • Autumn Forest: Hit the narrow light-blue target with Slingshot in the tent minigame to win Blue Bow, which automatically uses an oil vial to heal you if you would otherwise die. For Doll’s Head, use Climb to reach the top of the orb puzzle branch ahead of the orb, then hit it up into the pillar. Doll’s Head increases attack reach. See entries 19 and 20.
  • Night Garden: Glass Shard requires no extra ability. Overhealing extends your temporary damage boost. See entry 15.
  • The Docks: Nails with Twine requires Climb and Hover and makes enemies explode on death. Postage Stamp also requires Slingshot and improves currency rewards for consecutive kills without taking damage. See entries 23 and 24.
  • Dollmaker’s House and the Bog: Bird-Shaped Button requires Climb and Hover and boosts currency at low health. Scrap of Fabric requires no extra ability and slightly boosts aerial damage. See entries 25 and 26.
  • Webdrench Inn and Burial Vault: Insect Wing requires Hover and extends it. Ember Vial also requires Climb and makes Flaming Vessels emit embers before shattering. Rusted Compass requires Endless Climb and signals collectibles in the room. See entries 27–29.
  • The Drains and Hunter’s Cabin: Fish Hook requires Endless Climb and Hover and speeds vessel refill at low health. Rusty Fork requires Wall Sling and Climb and slightly boosts damage at low health. See entries 31 and 32.
  • Weathered Coin: Buy it from Looter for 600 at its listed price. Enemies drop a little more currency with it equipped.
  • Looter’s other four trinkets: Silver Ring (800) slightly boosts damage. Bottle Cap (250) makes Flaming Vessels bounce and is used for the Bouncing Bottles achievement. Skull Keychain (350) releases embers when you take damage. Rusted Thimble (550) reduces spike damage. These are prices before discounts.
  • Spirit return rewards: Bring rescued spirits back to the Night Garden nest. At 2 returned, Tiny Witch Sculpture replenishes a Flaming Vessel when you heal. At 12, Pearl Jewelry replenishes one when you take damage. At 20, Paper Clip with String increases Flaming Vessel damage. All three count toward the 32 trinkets needed for the western door in the Queen’s chambers.
