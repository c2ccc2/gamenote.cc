Workshop Token cleanup routes

Easy-to-miss Workshop Tokens
Area or questHow to collect
SpaAfter Willow announces the spa, head left from the tent near the Night Garden spirit nest to enter it. Check above the baths.
Dollmaker’s House time trialAfter clearing the trial, go straight into the reward room for the token. Return to the trial device and drop beside it to resume the main route.

Queen's CastleGo up two rooms from the left tent. Use Endless Climb and Hover to get above the token in the center of the room, then drop onto it.

Collection Notes

  • The reference checklist records 27 tokens. Spend tokens and currency at the tent workshop on upgradeable trinkets.
  • Look for bronze pentagons with a hammer; check each trinket's upgrade level.
  • Workshop unlock: Rescue the frogs after the Drains boss. When the red exclamation mark appears at a tent, speak to Willow inside for the first token (illustrated entry 1).
  • Pillar mechanism (illustrated entry 5): Fire the matchstick into the left side of the pillar. Raise the pillar, then use the matchstick to reach the token above.
  • Bubble steps (map entry 7): Stand in the middle and pop the bubbles with Slingshot. Move up one step after each shot until you reach the top.
  • Below the platforms (map entry 9): Drop from platform to platform and check the small hidden space at the bottom. Use the numbered entries in the linked checklist to open the room maps.
  • 1: Climb the side of the cage to drop it and open the path.
  • 2: Ring the lower bell. Before the platforms vanish, jump onto the wooden post and climb up.
  • 3: Strike the ceiling lever to open the door. 5: Check the hidden room.
  • 8: Enter the bubble, launch diagonally and jump onto the ledge. 10: Bring Hover.
  • 11: Climb the west wall, plant the matchstick with Wall Sling and launch onto the upper ledge. 12: Clear the platforming challenge. 13: Check the top of the spa.
  • 14: Use Slingshot to reach the wall above, then climb. 15: Bring Wall Sling for the climb. 16: Enter the bubble shaft and use a downward attack to drop to the bottom.
  • 17: Clear the time trial to open the room. 18: Climb the west wall, then follow the hidden shaft. 19: Check the hidden passage.
  • 20: Drop to extend the platform by the western nook. Dodge it, then follow it as it retracts. 21: Break the wall. 22: Clear the platforming challenge.
  • 23: Ring the first bell and jump the gap. Use the web sling to reach the second bell and strike it midair. Hover back to the sling, then launch upward.
  • 24: Check the alcove in the middle of the room. 25: Use Wall Sling to reach the platform. 26: Cross the spike gauntlet. 27: Clear the platforming challenge.
