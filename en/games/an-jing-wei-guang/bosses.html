Boss / encounter Index

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

What to do after a boss fight

BossWhat to do after victoryGuide
The GroundskeeperLeave the arena to the right for Hunter’s Cabin. Speak to Looter for the Flaming Vessel, then continue deeper for Slingshot.Open guide
The SwarmDrop to the right of the arena and learn Climb at the lower nest.Open guide
The Lady of the DrainsContinue to the next room for the Golden Feather. When a red exclamation mark appears at a tent, speak to Willow inside for the first Workshop Token.Open guide
Lida the TerribleGo to the next room on the left and speak to Egbert to complete the rescue. Drop through the floor beyond him to reach Midnight Drench.Open guide
Shrine of the Seven SonsGo left for the Golden Feather, then open the shortcut to Desiccated Castle.Open guide
StarsingerPick up the Golden Feather in the next room to the right.Open guide
Forest KingGo right into the next room, speak to Ilda and collect the Golden Feather. Before leaving, check the hidden passage on her right for a story page.Open guide
Creature from the DeepContinue to the campfire and finish the conversation with Barge to unlock water vessels, then return to the water to board.Open guide
The ColonyFinish the twins’ rescue and reunion, then return to Barge at the Docks shipyard for Endless Climb.Open guide
Dollmaker's Mirror / The DollmakerGo west to rescue Magda, then enter the ability nest to learn Wall Sling.Open guide
GravediggerContinue left with Irma to the birthday party and finish the scene to receive the Golden Feather.Open guide
Silver DollKeep going right and strike the lever beside the cage to free one sibling pair. Use the rescue guide to check the other two cages.Open guide
The QueenSpeak to everyone present to finish the celebration. Return above the arena, speak to Looter and strike the ceiling lever. Pick up the Queen’s Coin. For the true ending, collect all 32 trinkets to open the western door.Open guide
