The Groundskeeper

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

Area

Night Garden

Observed attack scenes

Pink petal-shaped projectiles appear above the Groundskeeper. Watch their direction.

★ Encounter notes

Continue through Night Garden after the oil-bottle tutorial to encounter the Groundskeeper.

After the encounter

After defeating the Groundskeeper, continue toward Hunter's Cabin for the next exploration segment.

Battle Notes

  • Watch where the seeds will land. When rakes follow from the sides, wait for them to get close before sliding through. Watch for the next rake while seeds are still falling.
  • When the Groundskeeper walks back and forth with leaves around him, slide through as he gets close. Below half health the leaves form two rings and the seeds and rakes get denser. Leave time for the slide to recover.
  • After the ground slam, wait until the shockwave is close before sliding through. Step in for a few matchstick hits after the attack, then back away.

After victory

Leave the arena to the right for Hunter’s Cabin. Speak to Looter for the Flaming Vessel, then continue deeper for Slingshot.
