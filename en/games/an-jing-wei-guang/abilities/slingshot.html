Slingshot

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-11-16

Effect

Launch a charged matchstick from firm ground and use it as a foothold for extra height.

Acquisition

Go right from the central Hunter’s Cabin tent to the mailbox room and ride the moving cage down. At the bottom, head right past the falling trap below the hanging cage and drop through the gap. Jump across the next set of cages and drop again. Follow the passage to the nest, take the floating Ability Egg and finish the Slingshot tutorial.
