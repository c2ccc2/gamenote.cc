Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

Quest starting points and next steps

Quest guideWhere to startWhat it leads to
Repair the ElevatorBroken lift beside the central Drains tent.Learn Wind Ride and collect three parts to reach the Lady of the Drains.
Graven Valley RescueCeremony entrance below the Graven Valley tent.Get the ticket, defeat Lida and rescue Egbert to continue into Midnight Drench.
Missing SailorsArena beyond the three Docks gate stones.Unlock vessels, rescue the twins in the Deep and return to the shipyard for Endless Climb.
A Ride to the PartyHead left from the Burial Vault entrance to find Irma.Defeat the Gravedigger and continue left to the party for a Golden Feather.
The Castle GateGate in the northern Bog.Bring seven Golden Feathers and show them to the guard at the top of the climb.
The Queen's ChamberSpeak to Ilda near the first tent after entering the castle.Rescue three sibling pairs and return to Ilda to open the route to the Queen.
