Time Trial

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

Open a trial guide by area. After finishing, enter the reward room to collect the prize. Use the reward column to check for missing collectibles.

Restarting and extra time

After a mistake, hold the input shown in the bottom-left corner to restart. Hold Y with the Xbox layout, and follow the on-screen prompt for other controls.

To practise the route, add time to trials in the gameplay settings. If the camera falls behind on an older build, update the game. The first Steam patch fixed a tracking issue in some trials at wide zoom settings.
