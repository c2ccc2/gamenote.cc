Bouncing Bottles

Guide editor: GAME NOTE · Updated 2026-10-13

Juggle a flaming vessel 10 times without it breaking

Category

Challenges

Battle Notes

  • Buy Bottle Cap for 250 currency at Looter’s tent shop and equip it. For a practice spot, look for flat ground with room overhead near a Midnight Drench tent.
  • Throw a Flaming Vessel straight up and stay underneath. Wait until it falls within matchstick range, then strike upward to send it back up. Land ten hits on the same vessel.
  • If the vessel drifts, take a short step beneath it before swinging. You can recover after a wall or floor bounce if it survives, though the rebound is harder to control. Start with a fresh vessel if it breaks, and refill at the tent when you run out.
